1. Arriving on Purgatory's shore, Dante and Virgil met the guardian Cato, who instructed Virgil to cleanse Dante with dew and gird him with a humble reed before their ascent.
2. An angel delivers new souls to Purgatory's shore, where Dante reunites with his friend Casella, whose song is interrupted by Cato's sharp rebuke, scattering the lingering spirits towards the mountain.
3. After Virgil explains his nature as a shade, the poets meet the excommunicated, where King Manfred recounts his salvation and asks Dante to seek prayers to shorten his penance.
4. After a difficult climb, Virgil explains southern hemisphere astronomy to Dante before they encounter the indolent Belacqua, who reveals he must wait a lifetime to begin his penance.
5. Dante encounters the souls of those who died violently and repented at the last moment, hearing the personal stories of Jacopo del Cassero, Bonconte da Montefeltro, and Pia de' Tolomei.
6. After Virgil explains prayer's power, he and Dante meet the poet Sordello, whose patriotic embrace sparks Dante's passionate invective against the political decay of Italy and Florence.
7. After greeting Virgil, Sordello explains that they cannot climb at night and leads the poets to the Valley of the Princes, where he points out the souls of several late-repentant European rulers.
8. In the Valley of the Rulers, Dante witnesses angels repel a serpent before speaking with Nino Visconti and Currado Malaspina, who prophesies Dante's future welcome by his family.
9. After dreaming of an eagle, Dante is carried by Saint Lucia to Purgatory's gate, where an angel inscribes seven 'P's on his forehead and opens the way for his purification.
10. On the first terrace of Purgatory, Dante and Virgil view divine marble carvings of humility before witnessing the proud souls crushed beneath heavy stones as they begin their penance.
11. On the terrace of the proud, Dante speaks with the souls Omberto and Oderisi, who discuss the sin of pride and the vanity of fame, using Provenzan Salvani's humility as a key example.
12. After observing carvings of punished pride, Dante is cleansed of that sin by an angel who erases a 'P' from his forehead, allowing him to feel lighter as he ascends the next stairway.
13. On the second terrace, Dante observes the envious souls with their eyes sewn shut and speaks with Sapia of Siena, who recounts her sin and her path to repentance.
14. Dante meets Guido del Duca, who laments the moral decay of Romagna and the Arno valley, before Virgil explains that disembodied voices of punished envy serve as a divine bridle against sin.
15. Guided by the Angel of Mercy, Dante ascends to the third terrace where, after Virgil's discourse on shared good and visions of meekness, a blinding smoke engulfs the poets.
16. On the terrace of the wrathful, Marco the Lombard explains to Dante that the world's corruption stems from humanity's free will and the Church's usurpation of temporal power, not from celestial influence.
17. After viewing visions of punished wrath, Dante ascends to the fourth terrace where Virgil begins his central discourse on love as the seed of every virtue and sin.
18. After Virgil discourses on love and free will, the souls of the slothful run past shouting examples of their sin and virtue before Dante, overwhelmed with thought, falls asleep.
19. After a dream about a deceptive Siren, the narrator ascends to the fifth terrace and speaks with Pope Adrian V, who explains the prostrate punishment for the avaricious.
20. Dante meets Hugh Capet, who denounces his avaricious descendants, before the entire mountain trembles and the souls cry out "Gloria in excelsis Deo," leaving Dante profoundly bewildered.
21. Dante and Virgil meet the newly purified poet Statius, who explains the mountain's recent trembling and is overcome with emotion upon learning his guide is his poetic hero, Virgil.
22. Statius reveals that Virgil's poetry led him from prodigality to secret Christianity before the poets reach the terrace of gluttony, where a voice from a tree recites examples of temperance.
23. On the terrace of the gluttonous, Dante recognizes his emaciated friend Forese Donati, who explains their willing punishment, praises his wife's prayers, and learns of Dante's journey with Virgil.
24. Dante discusses poetry with Bonagiunta, hears Forese prophesy his brother's violent death, and passes a second tree before an angel of temperance erases the sixth P from his forehead, clearing his way to ascend.
25. Statius explains the origin of the soul and how shades can feel hunger, after which the poets reach the final terrace where the lustful are purged by fire.
26. On the seventh terrace, Dante meets two groups of lustful souls and speaks with the poets Guido Guinizzelli and Arnaut Daniel, who explain their penance before disappearing into the fire.
27. The narrator passes through the wall of fire motivated by Beatrice, dreams of Leah and Rachel, and is then crowned sovereign over his own free will by Virgil, whose guidance is now complete.
28. Entering the Earthly Paradise, the narrator meets Matelda, who explains the divine nature of the forest, its wind, and its two rivers, revealing it to be the Golden Age dreamed of by ancient poets.
29. In the Earthly Paradise, Dante witnesses a magnificent divine procession led by seven candelabra, which includes biblical elders, symbolic creatures, and a triumphal chariot drawn by a griffin.
30. As Virgil vanishes, Beatrice appears and sternly rebukes the weeping narrator by name, demanding he repent for straying from the true path after her death.
31. After confessing his sins to Beatrice, the narrator is cleansed in the river Lethe and brought before her, where he witnesses a divine miracle in her eyes before she finally unveils her radiant smile.
32. After the Griffin ties the Chariot to the Tree of Knowledge, Dante witnesses a complex allegory depicting the corruption of the Church, which Beatrice commands him to record upon his return to the world.
33. After hearing Beatrice's final prophecy, Dante completes his purification by drinking from the river Eunoë, feeling wholly renewed and ready to ascend to the stars.
