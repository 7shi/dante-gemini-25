Purgatorio — Canto 10

Dante e Virgilio raggiungono la prima cornice, dove Dante contempla esempi divini di umiltà scolpiti nel marmo e vede le anime dei superbi, curvate sotto enormi sassi per espiare il loro peccato. Dante and Virgil reach the first terrace, where Dante contemplates divine examples of humility carved in marble and sees the souls of the proud, bent beneath enormous stones to atone for their sin. ダンテとウェルギリウスは第一の環道に着き、そこでダンテは大理石に彫られた神聖な謙遜の模範を眺め、罪を償うため巨大な石の下で身を曲げる傲慢な者たちの魂を見る。

1 Poi fummo dentro al soglio de la porta Then we were inside the threshold of the gate やがて我らは門の敷居の内側に入り、
2 che ’l mal amor de l’anime disusa, that the evil love of souls makes unused, 魂たちの邪悪な愛が使わなくするその門の、
3 perché fa parer dritta la via torta, because it makes the crooked way seem straight, なぜならそれは曲がった道を真っ直ぐに見せるからだ、
4 sonando la senti’ esser richiusa; sounding, I heard it being reclosed; 音を立ててそれが再び閉ざされるのを聞いた。
5 e s’io avesse li occhi vòlti ad essa, and if I had turned my eyes toward it, そしてもし私がそれに目を向けていたならば、
6 qual fora stata al fallo degna scusa? what would have been a worthy excuse for the fault? その過ちに対して、いかなる弁解が許されたであろうか？
7 Noi salavam per una pietra fessa, We were climbing through a cleft rock, 我らは裂けた岩を登っていった、
8 che si moveva e d’una e d’altra parte, that moved from one and the other side, それは一方からそして他方へと動き、
9 sì come l’onda che fugge e s’appressa. like the wave that flees and approaches. 逃げては近づく波のように。
10 «Qui si conviene usare un poco d’arte», "Here it is fitting to use a little skill," 「ここでは少々、術を用いるのがふさわしい」
11 cominciò ’l duca mio, «in accostarsi my leader began, "in keeping close 私の導き手は言った、「近づくにあたって
12 or quinci, or quindi al lato che si parte». now here, now there to the side that recedes." 今はこちら、次はあちらへと、退く側に」。
13 E questo fece i nostri passi scarsi, And this made our steps scarce, そしてこれは我らの歩みを乏しいものとした、
14 tanto che pria lo scemo de la luna so that before the waning of the moon そのため、欠けてゆく月が
15 rigiunse al letto suo per ricorcarsi, had rejoined its bed to lie down again, 再び横たわるためにその寝床に戻るよりも早く、
16 che noi fossimo fuor di quella cruna; we were out of that needle’s eye; 我らはその針の穴から外に出たのであった。
17 ma quando fummo liberi e aperti but when we were free and in the open しかし我らが自由になり開けた場所に出た時、
18 sù dove il monte in dietro si rauna, up where the mountain draws back together, 山が後ろに身を引くその高みで、
19 ïo stancato e amendue incerti I tired and both of us uncertain 私は疲れ果て、二人とも不確かであった
20 di nostra via, restammo in su un piano of our way, we stopped on a level place 我らの道について、我らはある平地に留まった、
21 solingo più che strade per diserti. more solitary than roads through deserts. 砂漠の道よりも孤独な。
22 Da la sua sponda, ove confina il vano, From its edge, where the void borders it, その岸から、虚空と境を接する場所から、
23 al piè de l’alta ripa che pur sale, to the foot of the high cliff that still rises, なおも登る高い崖の麓まで、
24 misurrebbe in tre volte un corpo umano; would measure three times a human body; 人間の体を三つ分測るであろう。
25 e quanto l’occhio mio potea trar d’ale, and as far as my eye could take wing, そして私の目が翼を広げられる限り、
26 or dal sinistro e or dal destro fianco, now on the left and now on the right flank, 今や左の、そして今や右の側面へと、
27 questa cornice mi parea cotale. this terrace seemed to me so. この環道は私にはそのように見えた。
28 Là sù non eran mossi i piè nostri anco, Our feet had not yet moved up there, そこでは我々の足はまだ動いていなかった、
29 quand’ io conobbi quella ripa intorno when I perceived that the bank around, その時私は周りのその崖が
30 che dritto di salita aveva manco, which was lacking a sheer slope for ascent, まっすぐ登る道を欠いていた、
31 esser di marmo candido e addorno was of white marble and adorned 真っ白な大理石でできており、飾られていることを知った
32 d’intagli sì, che non pur Policleto, with such carvings that not only Polyclitus, その彫刻たるや、ポリュクレイトスのみならず、
33 ma la natura lì avrebbe scorno. but nature herself would there be shamed. 自然でさえもそこに恥じ入るだろう。
34 L’angel che venne in terra col decreto The angel who came to earth with the decree その布告をもって地上に来た天使は
35 de la molt’ anni lagrimata pace, of the peace wept for through many years, 長年涙されてきた平和の、
36 ch’aperse il ciel del suo lungo divieto, which opened heaven from its long ban, その長い禁を解いて天を開いた、
37 dinanzi a noi pareva sì verace appeared before us so vividly 我々の前に実に真に迫って見え、
38 quivi intagliato in un atto soave, carved there in a gentle attitude, 優美な仕草でそこに彫られていた、
39 che non sembiava imagine che tace. that he did not seem a silent image. 黙する像とは思われなかった。
40 Giurato si saria ch’el dicesse ‘Ave!’; One would have sworn that he was saying ‘Ave!’; 彼が「アヴェ！」と言っていると誓われただろう；
41 perché iv’ era imaginata quella because there was imaged she なぜならそこにはあの方が描かれていたからだ
42 ch’ad aprir l’alto amor volse la chiave; who turned the key to open the high love; 高き愛を開くために鍵を回した；
43 e avea in atto impressa esta favella and had in her attitude impressed these words, そしてその姿にはこの言葉が刻まれていた
44 ‘Ecce ancilla Deï’, propriamente ‘Ecce ancilla Dei,’ as precisely 「見よ、神のはしためを」、まさしく
45 come figura in cera si suggella. as a figure is stamped on wax. 蝋に印が押されるように。
46 «Non tener pur ad un loco la mente», "Do not keep your mind on one place only," 「一つの場所にだけ心を留めるな」、
47 disse ’l dolce maestro, che m’avea said the sweet master, who had me と優しい師は言った、彼は私を
48 da quella parte onde ’l cuore ha la gente. on that side where people have their heart. 人々が心臓を持つ側に置いていた。
49 Per ch’i’ mi mossi col viso, e vedea Wherefore I moved my gaze, and saw それゆえ私は顔を動かし、そして見た
50 di retro da Maria, da quella costa behind Mary, on that side マリアの後ろに、その側面に
51 onde m’era colui che mi movea, where he who was moving me was, 私を促した方がいた、
52 un’altra storia ne la roccia imposta; another story set into the rock; 岩に刻まれたもう一つの物語を；
53 per ch’io varcai Virgilio, e fe’mi presso, so I passed Virgil, and drew near, それゆえ私はウェルギリウスを通り過ぎ、近づいた、
54 acciò che fosse a li occhi miei disposta. so that it might be presented to my eyes. それが私の目に映るようにと。
55 Era intagliato lì nel marmo stesso There was carved in the same marble 同じ大理石にそこに彫られていた
56 lo carro e ’ buoi, traendo l’arca santa, the cart and the oxen, drawing the holy ark, 車と牛が、聖櫃を曳いている、
57 per che si teme officio non commesso. which makes one fear an uncommissioned office. そのため委ねられていない務めを人は恐れる。
58 Dinanzi parea gente; e tutta quanta, Before it appeared people; and the whole crowd, 前には人々が見えた。そしてそのすべてが、
59 partita in sette cori, a’ due mie’ sensi divided into seven choirs, made my two senses 七つの合唱隊に分かれ、私の二つの感覚に
60 faceva dir l’un ‘No’, l’altro ‘Sì, canta’. —one say ‘No,’ the other ‘Yes, they sing.’ 一方は「否」、他方は「然り、歌っている」と言わせた。
61 Similemente al fummo de li ’ncensi Likewise with the smoke of the incense 同様に、香の煙に
62 che v’era imaginato, li occhi e ’l naso that was imaged there, the eyes and the nose そこに描かれていた、目と鼻は
63 e al sì e al no discordi fensi. became discordant between yes and no. 然りと否とで不一致をきたした。
64 Lì precedeva al benedetto vaso, There, preceding the blessed vessel, そこで祝福された器に先んじていた、
65 trescando alzato, l’umile salmista, dancing with robes uplifted, was the humble psalmist, 舞い上がりながら踊る、かの謙虚な詩篇作者は、
66 e più e men che re era in quel caso. and in that instance was both more and less than king. そしてその時、王以上でも以下でもあった。
67 Di contra, effigïata ad una vista Opposite, portrayed at a window 反対側では、ある窓辺に描かれて
68 d’un gran palazzo, Micòl ammirava of a great palace, Michal was looking on 大いなる宮殿の、ミカルは見つめていた
69 sì come donna dispettosa e trista. like a woman scornful and sad. 軽蔑し悲しむ女のように。
70 I’ mossi i piè del loco dov’ io stava, I moved my feet from the place where I was standing, 私は立っていた場所から足を動かした、
71 per avvisar da presso un’altra istoria, to look closely at another story, 近くで別の物語を確かめるために、
72 che di dietro a Micòl mi biancheggiava. that gleamed white behind Michal. ミカルの後ろで白く輝いていた。
73 Quiv’ era storïata l’alta gloria There was storied the high glory そこにはかの高き栄光が物語られていた
74 del roman principato, il cui valore of the Roman prince, whose worth ローマの君主の、その徳が
75 mosse Gregorio a la sua gran vittoria; moved Gregory to his great victory; グレゴリウスをその大いなる勝利へと動かした；
76 i’ dico di Traiano imperadore; I speak of the Emperor Trajan; 私が言うのは皇帝トラヤヌスのことだ；
77 e una vedovella li era al freno, and a poor little widow was at his bridle, そして一人の貧しい寡婦が彼の手綱の傍らにいた、
78 di lagrime atteggiata e di dolore. in an attitude of tears and of grief. 涙と悲しみの姿で。
79 Intorno a lui parea calcato e pieno Around him it seemed trampled and full 彼の周りは踏み固められ満ちているように見えた
80 di cavalieri, e l’aguglie ne l’oro of knights, and the eagles in gold 騎士たちで、そして金の鷲の旗が
81 sovr’ essi in vista al vento si movieno. above them visibly moved in the wind. 彼らの上で風に動いているように見えた。
82 La miserella intra tutti costoro The poor little woman among all of these その哀れな女は皆の中で
83 pareva dir: «Segnor, fammi vendetta seemed to say: “Lord, give me vengeance 言っているようだった。「主よ、私に仇討ちをしてください
84 di mio figliuol ch’è morto, ond’ io m’accoro»; for my son who is dead, for which I grieve”; 殺された我が息子の、それゆえ私は悲しんでいます」；
85 ed elli a lei rispondere: «Or aspetta and he to her to answer: “Now wait そして彼が彼女に答える。「さて、待て
86 tanto ch’i’ torni»; e quella: «Segnor mio», until I return”; and she: “My lord,” 私が戻るまで」；そして彼女は、「我が主よ」、
87 come persona in cui dolor s’affretta, like a person in whom grief is urgent, 悲しみが急き立てる人のように、
88 «se tu non torni?»; ed ei: «Chi fia dov’ io, “if you do not return?”; and he: “Who is in my place, 「もしあなたがお戻りにならなかったら？」；そして彼は、「私の後を継ぐ者が、
89 la ti farà»; ed ella: «L’altrui bene will do it for you”; and she: “What is another’s good あなたのためにそれを為すだろう」；そして彼女は、「他者の善行が
90 a te che fia, se ’l tuo metti in oblio?»; to you, if you forget your own?”; あなたに何となりましょう、もしご自身のそれを忘れるなら？」；
91 ond’ elli: «Or ti conforta; ch’ei convene wherefore he: “Now be comforted; for it is meet それゆえ彼は、「さて、心を安んぜよ；なぜならそれはふさわしいことだ
92 ch’i’ solva il mio dovere anzi ch’i’ mova: that I fulfill my duty before I move: 私が出立する前に私の義務を果たすことが：
93 giustizia vuole e pietà mi ritene». justice wills it and pity holds me back.” 正義がそれを望み、慈悲が私を引き留める」。
94 Colui che mai non vide cosa nova He who never saw a new thing かつて新しきものを見たことのない方が
95 produsse esto visibile parlare, produced this visible speech, この見える言葉を創り出した、
96 novello a noi perché qui non si trova. new to us because its like is not found here. 我々には新しい、なぜならここでは見出されないからだ。
97 Mentr’ io mi dilettava di guardare While I was delighting to look at 私が見ることに喜びを感じている間
98 l’imagini di tante umilitadi, the images of such great humilities, 多くの謙遜の像を、
99 e per lo fabbro loro a veder care, and dear to see because of their Maker, そしてその創り主ゆえに貴く見えた、
100 «Ecco di qua, ma fanno i passi radi», «Look over here, but they take slow steps», 「こちらだ、だが足取りはまばらだ」
101 mormorava il poeta, «molte genti: murmured the poet, «are many people: と詩人は呟いた、「多くの民がいる、
102 questi ne ’nvïeranno a li alti gradi». these will direct us to the high terraces». 彼らが我らを高き階へと導いてくれるだろう」。
103 Li occhi miei, ch’a mirare eran contenti My eyes, which were content in gazing 私の目は、眺めることに満足し、
104 per veder novitadi ond’ e’ son vaghi, to see new things of which they are fond, 新しいものを、それが好むものを見るために、
105 volgendosi ver’ lui non furon lenti. in turning toward him were not slow. 彼の方を向くのに遅れはしなかった。
106 Non vo’ però, lettor, che tu ti smaghi I do not wish, however, reader, that you be dismayed しかし読者よ、君が落胆してほしくはない、
107 di buon proponimento per udire from your good resolve on hearing 善き決意を失うことを、神が負債をいかに支払わせるかを
108 come Dio vuol che ’l debito si paghi. how God wills that the debt be paid. 聞いて。
109 Non attender la forma del martìre: Do not focus on the form of the torment: 苦しみの姿に目を向けるな、
110 pensa la succession; pensa ch’al peggio think of what follows; think that at worst その先を考えよ。最悪でも
111 oltre la gran sentenza non può ire. beyond the great sentence it cannot go. 大いなる審判を越えて続くことはないと考えよ。
112 Io cominciai: «Maestro, quel ch’io veggio I began: «Master, that which I see 私は始めた、「師よ、私が見るあれは
113 muovere a noi, non mi sembian persone, moving toward us does not seem to me like people, 我らに向かって動いてきますが、人には見えず、
114 e non so che, sì nel veder vaneggio». and I know not what, so much in my seeing am I deluded». 何なのか、見る目がくらんでいるようです」。
115 Ed elli a me: «La grave condizione And he to me: «The grievous condition すると彼が私に、「彼らの苦しみの
116 di lor tormento a terra li rannicchia, of their torment hunches them to the earth, 過酷な様が、彼らを地に屈ませているのだ、
117 sì che ’ miei occhi pria n’ebber tencione. so that my own eyes at first had a contest with it. ゆえに私の目も最初は見分けるのに苦労した。
118 Ma guarda fiso là, e disviticchia But look intently there, and disentangle だが、そこをじっと見つめ、その石の下に
119 col viso quel che vien sotto a quei sassi: with your sight what comes beneath those stones: 来るものを、視線で解きほぐすがいい。
120 già scorger puoi come ciascun si picchia». already you can perceive how each one beats himself». すでに各々がいかに打ちのめされているか見えるだろう」。
121 O superbi cristian, miseri lassi, O proud Christians, miserable and weary, おお、傲慢なるキリスト教徒よ、惨めで哀れな者よ、
122 che, de la vista de la mente infermi, who, infirm in the vision of the mind, 心の視力を病み、
123 fidanza avete ne’ retrosi passi, place your trust in backward steps, 後ろ向きの歩みに信頼を置く者よ、
124 non v’accorgete voi che noi siam vermi do you not perceive that we are worms 気づかぬのか、我らが蛆虫であることを、
125 nati a formar l’angelica farfalla, born to form the angelic butterfly, 天使の蝶を形作るために生まれ、
126 che vola a la giustizia sanza schermi? that flies to justice without defenses? 妨げなく正義へと飛んでいくというのに。
127 Di che l’animo vostro in alto galla, Of what does your spirit float on high, 何ゆえに汝らの魂は高みに浮かぶのか、
128 poi siete quasi antomata in difetto, since you are almost defective automata, 欠陥のある未完成の虫けらのようなものなのに、
129 sì come vermo in cui formazion falla? like a worm in which formation fails? 形を成すのに失敗した蛆虫のように。
130 Come per sostentar solaio o tetto, As to support a ceiling or a roof, 床や屋根を支えるために、
131 per mensola talvolta una figura for a corbel sometimes a figure 持ち送りとして、時折ある像が
132 si vede giugner le ginocchia al petto, is seen to join its knees to its chest, 膝を胸につけているのが見られるが、
133 la qual fa del non ver vera rancura which from the unreal makes real distress それは見る者のうちに、偽りから
134 nascere ’n chi la vede; così fatti arise in whoever sees it; made thus 真の苦悩を生じさせる。そのように
135 vid’ io color, quando puosi ben cura. I saw those souls, when I paid good heed. 作られた者たちを、私はよく注意して見た。
136 Vero è che più e meno eran contratti True it is that more and less they were contracted 確かに、彼らはより多く、またより少なく縮こまっていた、
137 secondo ch’avien più e meno a dosso; according to the more and less they had on their backs; 背負うものがより多く、またより少ないのに応じて。
138 e qual più pazïenza avea ne li atti, and he who had most patience in his acts, そしてその仕草に最も忍耐を示していた者も、
139 piangendo parea dicer: ‘Più non posso’. weeping seemed to say: ‘I can no more’. 泣きながら言うようだった、「もう耐えられない」と。
