Purgatorio — Canto 29

In the Earthly Paradise, Dante witnesses a magnificent divine procession led by seven candelabra, which includes biblical elders, symbolic creatures, and a triumphal chariot drawn by a griffin. 地上の楽園で、語り手は七つの燭台に導かれ、聖書とキリストを象徴する壮大な寓意的行列が目の前を進むのを目撃した。

1 Cantando come donna innamorata, Singing like a woman in love, 恋する女のように歌いながら、
2 continüò col fin di sue parole: she continued with the end of her words: 彼女は言葉の結びを続けた。
3 ‘Beati quorum tecta sunt peccata!’. ‘Beati quorum tecta sunt peccata!’. 「幸いなるかな、その罪を覆われたる者は！」と。
4 E come ninfe che si givan sole And like nymphs who went alone そして、一人で進みゆくニンフのように
5 per le salvatiche ombre, disïando through the savage shades, desiring 森の木陰を、ある者は太陽を
6 qual di veder, qual di fuggir lo sole, some to see, some to flee the sun, 見ようと望み、ある者は避けようと望みながら、
7 allor si mosse contra ’l fiume, andando then she moved against the river, going 彼女は川の流れに逆らって進み始めた、
8 su per la riva; e io pari di lei, up along the bank; and I parallel to her, 岸辺を遡り、そして私も彼女と並んで、
9 picciol passo con picciol seguitando. following small step with small step. 小さな歩みを小さな歩みで追っていった。
10 Non eran cento tra ’ suoi passi e ’ miei, There were not a hundred between her steps and mine, 彼女の歩みと私の歩みが百歩にも満たないうちに、
11 quando le ripe igualmente dier volta, when the banks equally made a turn, 両岸は同じように向きを変え、
12 per modo ch’a levante mi rendei. so that toward the east I was turned. その結果、私は東を向くことになった。
13 Né ancor fu così nostra via molta, Nor yet was our way thus very long, 私たちの道程はまだそれほど長くはなかった、
14 quando la donna tutta a me si torse, when the lady turned herself completely to me, その時、貴婦人は完全に私の方を向き、
15 dicendo: «Frate mio, guarda e ascolta». saying: “My brother, look and listen.” 言った。「わが兄弟よ、見よ、そして聞け」と。
16 Ed ecco un lustro sùbito trascorse And behold a sudden brightness ran そして見よ、突如として輝きが走り抜けた
17 da tutte parti per la gran foresta, from all parts through the great forest, 大いなる森の中を四方八方から、
18 tal che di balenar mi mise in forse. such that of lightning it put me in doubt. それは稲妻ではないかと私を疑わせた。
19 Ma perché ’l balenar, come vien, resta, But because lightning, as it comes, ceases, しかし稲妻は、現れては消えるものだが、
20 e quel, durando, più e più splendeva, and that, enduring, shone more and more, その光は、持続し、ますます輝きを増したので、
21 nel mio pensier dicea: ‘Che cosa è questa?’. in my thought I said: ‘What thing is this?’. 私は心の中で言った。「これは一体何だ？」と。
22 E una melodia dolce correva And a sweet melody was running そして甘美な旋律が流れた
23 per l’aere luminoso; onde buon zelo through the luminous air; whence good zeal 光り輝く大気の中を。それゆえに善き熱意が
24 mi fé riprender l’ardimento d’Eva, made me blame the audacity of Eve, 私にエヴァの不遜を非難させた、
25 che là dove ubidia la terra e ’l cielo, who there where earth and heaven obeyed, 天と地が従うその場所で、
26 femmina, sola e pur testé formata, a woman, alone and just now formed, 女であり、一人で、しかも創られたばかりなのに、
27 non sofferse di star sotto alcun velo; did not suffer to stay beneath any veil; いかなるヴェールの下にいることにも耐えなかった彼女の不遜を。
28 sotto ’l qual se divota fosse stata, under which if she had been devout, もしその下に敬虔でいたならば、
29 avrei quelle ineffabili delizie I would have those ineffable delights 私はあの言葉に尽くせぬ喜びを
30 sentite prima e più lunga fïata. felt sooner and for a longer time. もっと早く、そしてもっと長く感じていたであろうに。
31 Mentr’ io m’andava tra tante primizie While I was going among so many first fruits 私が永遠の喜びの
32 de l’etterno piacer tutto sospeso, of the eternal pleasure, completely rapt, 数々の初物に囲まれて、完全に心を奪われ、
33 e disïoso ancora a più letizie, and desirous still for more joys, そしてさらなる喜悦を望みながら歩いていると、
34 dinanzi a noi, tal quale un foco acceso, before us, just like an ignited fire, 私たちの前で、燃え盛る火のように、
35 ci si fé l’aere sotto i verdi rami; the air beneath the green branches became; 緑の枝々の下の大気がなった。
36 e ’l dolce suon per canti era già inteso. and the sweet sound as songs was now understood. そしてその甘美な音は、すでに歌声として聞き取れた。
37 O sacrosante Vergini, se fami, O sacrosanct Virgins, if hungers, おお、聖なる乙女たちよ、もし飢えが、
38 freddi o vigilie mai per voi soffersi, colds or vigils I ever suffered for you, 寒さや不眠が、かつて汝らのために耐えられたなら、
39 cagion mi sprona ch’io mercé vi chiami. a reason spurs me to call on you for reward. その故に私は汝らに褒美を乞う。
40 Or convien che Elicona per me versi, Now it is fitting that Helicon pour forth for me, 今こそヘリコンは我がために注ぎ、
41 e Uranìe m’aiuti col suo coro and that Urania aid me with her choir ウラニアはその合唱隊とともに我を助け、
42 forti cose a pensar mettere in versi. to put into verse things difficult to conceive. 詩句に込めるべき難事を思索するのを。
43 Poco più oltre, sette alberi d’oro A little further on, seven golden trees 少し先には、七本の黄金の木が、
44 falsava nel parere il lungo tratto were falsified in appearance by the long stretch 長い隔たりはその見かけを偽っていた、
45 del mezzo ch’era ancor tra noi e loro; of the space that was still between us and them; 我らとそれらの間にまだあった中間の。
46 ma quand’ i’ fui sì presso di lor fatto, but when I had come so close to them しかし、私がそれらに十分に近づいたとき、
47 che l’obietto comun, che ’l senso inganna, that the common object, which deceives the sense, 感覚を欺く共通の対象物が、
48 non perdea per distanza alcun suo atto, did not through distance lose any of its properties, 距離のせいでその真の姿を少しも失わなくなると、
49 la virtù ch’a ragion discorso ammanna, the faculty that furnishes discourse to reason 理性に言葉を与える力が、
50 sì com’ elli eran candelabri apprese, apprehended them to be candelabra, それらが燭台であることを理解した、
51 e ne le voci del cantare ‘Osanna’. and in the voices of the singing, ‘Hosanna’. そして歌声の中の言葉が「ホザンナ」であることも。
52 Di sopra fiammeggiava il bello arnese Above flamed the beautiful equipment, その上では、美しい装飾が燃え輝き、
53 più chiaro assai che luna per sereno far brighter than the moon in a clear sky 晴れた夜空の月よりもはるかに明るく、
54 di mezza notte nel suo mezzo mese. at midnight in the middle of her month. 真夜中の、月の半ばの。
55 Io mi rivolsi d’ammirazion pieno I turned around, full of admiration, 私は感嘆に満ちて振り返った、
56 al buon Virgilio, ed esso mi rispuose to the good Virgil, and he answered me 善きウェルギリウスの方へ、すると彼は私に答えた、
57 con vista carca di stupor non meno. with a look no less laden with amazement. 同じく驚きに満ちた眼差しで。
58 Indi rendei l’aspetto a l’alte cose Then I returned my gaze to the high things それから私は再び、その崇高なものへと視線を戻した、
59 che si movieno incontr’ a noi sì tardi, that were moving toward us so slowly それらは我らに向かってあまりにゆっくりと動いていたので、
60 che foran vinte da novelle spose. they would have been outpaced by new brides. 新しい花嫁たちにさえ追い越されるほどだった。
61 La donna mi sgridò: «Perché pur ardi The lady scolded me: “Why do you burn 貴婦人は私を叱った、「なぜあなたは燃えるのですか
62 sì ne l’affetto de le vive luci, so in the affection for the living lights, それほどまでに、その生ける光への愛情に、
63 e ciò che vien di retro a lor non guardi?». and not watch that which comes behind them?”. そしてその後ろから来るものを見ないのですか？」。
64 Genti vid’ io allor, come a lor duci, People I saw then, as if following their leaders, すると私は人々を見た、彼らの導き手に従うように、
65 venire appresso, vestite di bianco; coming after, dressed in white; 後ろからやって来るのを、白い衣をまとって。
66 e tal candor di qua già mai non fuci. and such whiteness was never on this side. そしてそのような白さは、この地上では決してなかった。
67 L’acqua imprendëa dal sinistro fianco, The water was taking from my left flank, 水は左の岸から（光景を）映し取り、
68 e rendea me la mia sinistra costa, and gave back to me my left side, そして私の左半身を私に返した、
69 s’io riguardava in lei, come specchio anco. if I looked in it, like a mirror also. 私がそれを覗き込むと、鏡のようにも。
70 Quand’ io da la mia riva ebbi tal posta, When on my bank I had such a position 私が岸辺でそのような位置を占めたとき、
71 che solo il fiume mi facea distante, that only the river made me distant, ただ川だけが私を隔てているほどの、
72 per veder meglio ai passi diedi sosta, to see better I gave a halt to my steps, よりよく見るために、私は歩みを止めた、
73 e vidi le fiammelle andar davante, and I saw the little flames go before, そして私は小さな炎が先に行くのを見た、
74 lasciando dietro a sé l’aere dipinto, leaving the air behind them painted, 後ろの大気を描きながら、
75 e di tratti pennelli avean sembiante; and they had the semblance of drawn brushstrokes; そしてそれらは引かれた絵筆のようであった。
76 sì che lì sopra rimanea distinto so that up there remained distinct そのため、その上にははっきりと残っていた、
77 di sette liste, tutte in quei colori seven stripes, all in those colors 七つの筋が、すべてあの色で
78 onde fa l’arco il Sole e Delia il cinto. with which the Sun makes his bow and Delia her girdle. 太陽が虹を作り、デリアがその帯を作る。
79 Questi ostendali in dietro eran maggiori These banners stretched further behind これらの旗印は後ろへと長く伸び、
80 che la mia vista; e, quanto a mio avviso, than my sight; and, as far as I could judge, 私の視界を超えていた。そして私の見立てでは、
81 diece passi distavan quei di fori. the outer ones were ten paces apart. 最も外側のもの同士は十歩離れていた。
82 Sotto così bel ciel com’ io diviso, Under such a beautiful sky as I describe, 私が描くかくも美しき空の下、
83 ventiquattro seniori, a due a due, twenty-four elders, two by two, 二十四人の長老が、二人ずつ、
84 coronati venien di fiordaliso. came crowned with fleurs-de-lis. 百合の花の冠を戴いてやって来た。
85 Tutti cantavan: «Benedicta tue All were singing: “Blessed art thou 皆が歌っていた、「汝は祝福されん」
86 ne le figlie d’Adamo, e benedette among the daughters of Adam, and blessed 「アダムの娘たちの中で、そして祝福されよ」
87 sieno in etterno le bellezze tue!». be your beauties eternally!”. 「永遠に汝の美しさは！」
88 Poscia che i fiori e l’altre fresche erbette After the flowers and the other fresh grasses 花々とその他の瑞々しい草々が、
89 a rimpetto di me da l’altra sponda opposite me on the other bank 対岸の私の正面で、
90 libere fuor da quelle genti elette, were free from that chosen people, その選ばれた人々から解放されると、
91 sì come luce luce in ciel seconda, just as light follows light in the heavens, 天で光が光に次ぐように、
92 vennero appresso lor quattro animali, there came after them four animals, 彼らの後に四頭の生き物がやって来た、
93 coronati ciascun di verde fronda. each one crowned with green leaves. それぞれ緑の葉の冠を戴いていた。
94 Ognuno era pennuto di sei ali; Each one was feathered with six wings; それぞれが六つの翼を持ち、
95 le penne piene d’occhi; e li occhi d’Argo, the feathers full of eyes; and the eyes of Argus, 羽根は眼で満ちていた。そしてアルゴスの眼も、
96 se fosser vivi, sarebber cotali. if they were alive, would be such as these. もし生きていたなら、このようであっただろう。
97 A descriver lor forme più non spargo To describe their forms I do not scatter more 彼らの姿を描写するためにもはや私は費やさない
98 rime, lettor; ch’altra spesa mi strigne, rhymes, reader; for another expense constrains me, 詩句を、読者よ。他の務めが私を急き立て、
99 tanto ch’a questa non posso esser largo; so much that on this I cannot be generous; これについては、私は贅沢にはなれないのだ。
100 ma leggi Ezechïel, che li dipigne but read Ezekiel, who depicts them だがエゼキエルを読め、彼は彼らを描いている、
101 come li vide da la fredda parte as he saw them from the cold region 彼が北の方角から
102 venir con vento e con nube e con igne; coming with wind and with cloud and with fire; 風と雲と火と共に来るのを見たように。
103 e quali i troverai ne le sue carte, and as you will find them in his pages, そして汝が彼の書物で見出すであろう姿、
104 tali eran quivi, salvo ch’a le penne such they were here, except that for the wings その姿はここでも同じであった、羽根は別だが。
105 Giovanni è meco e da lui si diparte. John is with me and from him it departs. ヨハネは私に与し、彼（エゼキエル）とは袂を分かつ。
106 Lo spazio dentro a lor quattro contenne The space within the four of them contained その四つの生き物の内側の空間には
107 un carro, in su due rote, trïunfale, a chariot, on two wheels, triumphal, 二つの車輪を持つ、一台の凱旋車があった、
108 ch’al collo d’un grifon tirato venne. which came drawn by the neck of a griffin. それはグリフォンの首に引かれてやって来た。
109 Esso tendeva in sù l’una e l’altra ale It extended upward its one and its other wing そいつは両の翼を上へと伸ばし、
110 tra la mezzana e le tre e tre liste, between the middle and the three and three bands, 中央の帯と三つずつの帯の間に差し入れていた、
111 sì ch’a nulla, fendendo, facea male. so that in splitting them, it did harm to none. そのため裂きながらも、いずれにも害をなさなかった。
112 Tanto salivan che non eran viste; They rose so high that they were not seen; それらはあまりに高く昇り、見えなかった。
113 le membra d’oro avea quant’ era uccello, its limbs were of gold where it was a bird, 鳥である部分は黄金の四肢を持ち、
114 e bianche l’altre, di vermiglio miste. and the others white, mixed with vermilion. 他の部分は白く、朱色を混ぜていた。
115 Non che Roma di carro così bello Not only did Rome with a chariot so beautiful ローマがこれほど美しい車で
116 rallegrasse Affricano, o vero Augusto, not gladden Africanus, or truly Augustus, アフリカヌス、あるいはアウグストゥスを喜ばせたこともなく、
117 ma quel del Sol saria pover con ello; but that of the Sun would be poor beside it; 太陽の車さえ、これと比べれば貧しいだろう。
118 quel del Sol che, svïando, fu combusto that of the Sun which, swerving, was burnt up その太陽の車とは、道を踏み外し、燃え尽きたもの、
119 per l’orazion de la Terra devota, by the prayer of the devout Earth, 敬虔なる大地の祈りによって、
120 quando fu Giove arcanamente giusto. when Jupiter was mysteriously just. ユピテルが不可解なまでに正しかった時のこと。
121 Tre donne in giro da la destra rota Three ladies in a circle by the right wheel 右の車輪の側では三人の貴婦人が輪になって
122 venian danzando; l’una tanto rossa came dancing; the one so red 踊りながらやって来た。一人はあまりに赤く、
123 ch’a pena fora dentro al foco nota; that she would scarcely be noted in the fire; 炎の中でもほとんど気づかれないほどだった。
124 l’altr’ era come se le carni e l’ossa the other was as if her flesh and bones もう一人は、まるでその肉も骨も
125 fossero state di smeraldo fatte; had been made of emerald; 翠玉でできているかのようだった。
126 la terza parea neve testé mossa; the third seemed like newly fallen snow; 三人目は、今しがた降った雪のようであった。
127 e or parëan da la bianca tratte, and now they seemed led by the white one, そして時に白い女に導かれているように見え、
128 or da la rossa; e dal canto di questa now by the red; and from the song of this last 時に赤い女に。そしてこの女の歌から
129 l’altre toglien l’andare e tarde e ratte. the others took their steps both slow and fast. 他の者たちは遅速の歩みを取っていた。
130 Da la sinistra quattro facean festa, At the left, four made a festival, 左手では四人の貴婦人が祝祭をなし、
131 in porpore vestite, dietro al modo clothed in purple, following the measure 紫の衣をまとい、その作法に従っていた
132 d’una di lor ch’avea tre occhi in testa. of one of them who had three eyes in her head. 彼女らのうちの一人、頭に三つの目を持つ者の。
133 Appresso tutto il pertrattato nodo After all the aforementioned group その語られた一団すべての後に
134 vidi due vecchi in abito dispari, I saw two old men in dissimilar dress, 私は異なる装いの二人の老人を見た、
135 ma pari in atto e onesto e sodo. but equal in their mien, both honest and firm. しかし振る舞いは同じく、実直で堅固であった。
136 L’un si mostrava alcun de’ famigliari The one showed himself as one of the familiars 一人は、あの至高のヒポクラテスの
137 di quel sommo Ipocràte che natura of that supreme Hippocrates whom nature 弟子の一人であることを示していた、ヒポクラテスは自然が
138 a li animali fé ch’ell’ ha più cari; made for the animals that she holds most dear; 最も愛する動物たちのために作った者だ。
139 mostrava l’altro la contraria cura the other showed the contrary concern もう一人は、逆の配慮を示していた
140 con una spada lucida e aguta, with a sword, shining and sharp, 輝き鋭い一本の剣で、
141 tal che di qua dal rio mi fé paura. such that on this side of the stream it made me afraid. それは川のこちら側にいる私をさえ恐怖させた。
142 Poi vidi quattro in umile paruta; Then I saw four of humble appearance; 次に私は、つつましい姿の四人を見た。
143 e di retro da tutti un vecchio solo and behind them all a single old man そして全員の後ろから、ただ一人の老人が
144 venir, dormendo, con la faccia arguta. come, sleeping, with a sharp-witted face. 眠りながら、鋭い顔つきでやって来た。
145 E questi sette col primaio stuolo And these seven with the first company そしてこの七人は、最初の集団と
146 erano abitüati, ma di gigli were attired, but not with a garland of lilies 同じ装いをしていたが、百合の花で
147 dintorno al capo non facëan brolo, around their heads did they make a garden, 頭の周りに花輪を作ってはいなかった、
148 anzi di rose e d’altri fior vermigli; but rather of roses and other vermilion flowers; むしろ薔薇や他の赤い花々で。
149 giurato avria poco lontano aspetto a gaze from not far off would have sworn 少し離れて見れば、誓って言えただろう
150 che tutti ardesser di sopra da’ cigli. that they all burned from above the eyebrows. 皆が眉の上から燃え上がっていると。
151 E quando il carro a me fu a rimpetto, And when the chariot was opposite me, そして車が私の正面に来た時、
152 un tuon s’udì, e quelle genti degne a thunder was heard, and those worthy people 一つの雷鳴が聞こえ、その尊い人々は
153 parvero aver l’andar più interdetto, seemed to have their progress further forbidden, 進むことをさらに禁じられたように見えた、
154 fermandosi ivi con le prime insegne. halting there with the foremost banners. 最初の印と共に、その場に停止して。
