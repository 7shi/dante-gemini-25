Paradiso — Canto 21

Nel cielo di Saturno, Beatrice prepara Dante alla visione della scala d’oro e delle anime contemplative, tra cui Pietro Damiano, che spiega il silenzio celeste, denuncia la corruzione dei prelati e suscita un grido incomprensibile. In the Heaven of Saturn, Beatrice prepares Dante for the vision of the golden ladder and the contemplative souls, including Peter Damian, who explains the celestial silence, denounces corrupt prelates, and prompts an incomprehensible cry. 土星天で、ベアトリーチェはダンテに黄金の梯子と観想する魂たち、なかでも天上の沈黙を説明し、腐敗した高位聖職者を非難して、理解できない叫びを引き起こすピエトロ・ダミアーノの幻を見せる準備をさせる。

1 Già eran li occhi miei rifissi al volto My eyes were already fixed again on the face 既に我が目は再び顔に向けられていた
2 de la mia donna, e l’animo con essi, of my lady, and my mind with them, 我が貴婦人の、そして心もそれと共に、
3 e da ogne altro intento s’era tolto. and from every other intent it was removed. そして他のあらゆる意図から離れていた。
4 E quella non ridea; ma «S’io ridessi», And she was not smiling; but "If I were to smile," そして彼女は微笑まなかった。だが「もし私が微笑めば」
5 mi cominciò, «tu ti faresti quale she began to me, "you would become such as と私に語り始めた、「あなたはなるでしょう
6 fu Semelè quando di cener fessi: was Semele when she was made of ashes: 灰と化した時のセメレのように。
7 ché la bellezza mia, che per le scale for my beauty, which along the stairs なぜなら私の美しさは、階段を
8 de l’etterno palazzo più s’accende, of the eternal palace more ignites, 永遠の宮殿の、より輝きを増すのです、
9 com’ hai veduto, quanto più si sale, as you have seen, the higher one ascends, あなたが見たように、より高く昇るほどに。
10 se non si temperasse, tanto splende, if it were not tempered, so shines, もし和らげられなければ、あまりに輝き、
11 che ’l tuo mortal podere, al suo fulgore, that your mortal power, to its splendor, あなたの定命の力は、その輝きに、
12 sarebbe fronda che trono scoscende. would be a branch that thunder splits. 雷鳴が打ち落とす木の葉となるでしょう。
13 Noi sem levati al settimo splendore, We are raised to the seventh splendor, 我らは第七の輝きへと昇りました、
14 che sotto ’l petto del Leone ardente which under the breast of the burning Lion それは燃え盛る獅子座の胸の下で
15 raggia mo misto giù del suo valore. now radiates down mixed with its power. 今やその力と混じり合って地上へ輝く。
16 Ficca di retro a li occhi tuoi la mente, Fix your mind behind your eyes, あなたの目の後ろに心を据え、
17 e fa di quelli specchi a la figura and make of them mirrors for the figure そして目を鏡となさい、その姿のための
18 che ’n questo specchio ti sarà parvente». that in this mirror will be apparent to you." この鏡の中にあなたに見えてくるであろう」。
19 Qual savesse qual era la pastura Whoever could know what was the pasture もし知る者があれば、いかなる糧であったかを
20 del viso mio ne l’aspetto beato of my gaze in that blessed aspect 我が視線の、祝福された姿における
21 quand’ io mi trasmutai ad altra cura, when I turned myself to another care, 私が別の務めへと移った時の、
22 conoscerebbe quanto m’era a grato would know how pleasing it was to me 知るであろう、いかに私に喜ばしかったかを
23 ubidire a la mia celeste scorta, to obey my celestial guide, 我が天の導き手に従うことが、
24 contrapesando l’un con l’altro lato. counterbalancing the one side with the other. 一方と他方を釣り合わせながら。
25 Dentro al cristallo che ’l vocabol porta, Inside the crystal which bears the name, その名を負う水晶の中、
26 cerchiando il mondo, del suo caro duce circling the world, of its dear leader 世界を巡り、その敬愛する指導者の
27 sotto cui giacque ogne malizia morta, under whom all malice lay dead, 治世下に一切の悪意が死に絶えた、
28 di color d’oro in che raggio traluce of golden color in which a ray shines through 光が透ける黄金色の
29 vid’ io uno scaleo eretto in suso I saw a ladder erected upward 梯子が天高く聳えるのを私は見た、
30 tanto, che nol seguiva la mia luce. so high, that my sight did not follow it. あまりの高さに私の視線は追いつかないほど。
31 Vidi anche per li gradi scender giuso I also saw descending down the steps また、その段を降りてくる
32 tanti splendor, ch’io pensai ch’ogne lume so many splendors, that I thought every light 無数の輝きを見たので、天に現れる
33 che par nel ciel, quindi fosse diffuso. that appears in heaven, was diffused from there. 全ての光は、ここから放たれているのだと思った。
34 E come, per lo natural costume, And as, by their natural custom, そして、自然の習性に従い、
35 le pole insieme, al cominciar del giorno, the rooks together, at the beginning of the day, コクマルガラスの群れが、夜明けと共に
36 si movono a scaldar le fredde piume; move to warm their cold feathers; 冷えた羽を温めようと動き出し、
37 poi altre vanno via sanza ritorno, then others go away without returning, あるものは帰ることなく飛び去り、
38 altre rivolgon sé onde son mosse, others turn themselves back to where they started, あるものは飛び立った場所へ引き返し、
39 e altre roteando fan soggiorno; and others, wheeling, make their stay; またあるものは旋回しながら留まるように、
40 tal modo parve me che quivi fosse such a manner it seemed to me was there そのように、ここでの輝きも
41 in quello sfavillar che ’nsieme venne, in that sparkling that came together, 一つに集まってきらめき、
42 sì come in certo grado si percosse. as soon as it struck a certain step. ある段にぶつかるように見えた。
43 E quel che presso più ci si ritenne, And the one that stopped nearest to us, そして、我々に最も近く留まったものは、
44 si fé sì chiaro, ch’io dicea pensando: became so bright, that I said in my thought: 実に輝きを増したので、私は心の中で言った、
45 ‘Io veggio ben l’amor che tu m’accenne. 'I see well the love that you signal to me. 「あなたが私に示す愛はよくわかります。
46 Ma quella ond’ io aspetto il come e ’l quando But she from whom I await the how and the when しかし、私が語ることと黙ることの
47 del dire e del tacer, si sta; ond’ io, of speaking and of keeping silent, is still; so I, いかに、いつを待つ彼女は静かにしており、だから私は、
48 contra ’l disio, fo ben ch’io non dimando’. against my desire, do well that I do not ask.' 望みに反して、尋ねないのがよいのだ」と。
49 Per ch’ella, che vedëa il tacer mio So she, who saw my silence すると彼女は、万物を見る方の眼差しの中に
50 nel veder di colui che tutto vede, in the sight of Him who sees all, 私の沈黙を見て、
51 mi disse: «Solvi il tuo caldo disio». said to me: “Release your warm desire.” 私に言った、「あなたの熱い願いを解き放ちなさい」。
52 E io incominciai: «La mia mercede And I began: “My own merit そこで私は始めた、「私の功徳は
53 non mi fa degno de la tua risposta; does not make me worthy of your reply; あなたの答えに値するものではありません。
54 ma per colei che ’l chieder mi concede, but for her who grants me leave to ask, しかし、尋ねることを許してくださる彼女のために、
55 vita beata che ti stai nascosta O blessed life that are hidden 祝福された生命よ、あなたの喜びに隠れている、
56 dentro a la tua letizia, fammi nota within your own joy, make known to me 私に知らせてください、どうして私にかくも近くに身を置いたのか
57 la cagion che sì presso mi t’ha posta; the reason that has placed you so near to me; その理由を。
58 e dì perché si tace in questa rota and tell me why in this wheel is silent そして、なぜこの天輪では
59 la dolce sinfonia di paradiso, the sweet symphony of paradise, 天国の甘美な交響曲が沈黙しているのか、
60 che giù per l’altre suona sì divota». which through the others below sounds so devoutly.” 他の天ではあれほど敬虔に響いていたのに」。
61 «Tu hai l’udir mortal sì come il viso», «You have mortal hearing just as you have mortal sight», 「お前は顔と同じく、聴覚も定命のもの」
62 rispuose a me; «onde qui non si canta he answered me; «for which reason there is no singing here と彼は私に答えた、「ゆえにここでは歌われない
63 per quel che Bëatrice non ha riso. for the same reason that Beatrice has not smiled. ベアトリーチェが微笑まなかったのと同じ理由で。
64 Giù per li gradi de la scala santa Down the rungs of the holy ladder この聖なる階の段を
65 discesi tanto sol per farti festa I descended this far only to give you welcome 私が下りてきたのは、ただお前を歓迎するため
66 col dire e con la luce che mi ammanta; with my speech and with the light that cloaks me; 言葉と、私を包む光とで。
67 né più amor mi fece esser più presta, nor did greater love make me swifter, より多くの愛が私をより速くさせたのではない、
68 ché più e tanto amor quinci sù ferve, for more and as much love burns up there from here, なぜなら、ここより上ではより多くの、そして同等の愛が燃え盛るのだから、
69 sì come il fiammeggiar ti manifesta. as the flaming makes manifest to you. その輝きがお前に示すように。
70 Ma l’alta carità, che ci fa serve But the high charity, which makes us servants だが、我らを僕とする高き慈愛が
71 pronte al consiglio che ’l mondo governa, ready for the counsel that governs the world, 世を統べる御心にすぐ従わせ、
72 sorteggia qui sì come tu osserve». allots here as you observe». お前が見るとおり、ここに御心を割り当てるのだ」。
73 «Io veggio ben», diss’ io, «sacra lucerna, «I see well», I said, «O sacred lamp, 「聖なる灯火よ、私はよくわかる」と私は言った、
74 come libero amore in questa corte how free love in this court 「この宮廷では自由な愛が
75 basta a seguir la provedenza etterna; suffices to follow eternal providence; 永遠の摂理に従うに足ることを。
76 ma questo è quel ch’a cerner mi par forte, but this is what seems hard for me to discern, だが、私が見分けるに困難に思われるのはこのことだ、
77 perché predestinata fosti sola why you alone were predestined なぜ、おんみ一人だけが定められたのか
78 a questo officio tra le tue consorte». to this office among your companions». 仲間の中から、この務めに」。
79 Né venni prima a l’ultima parola, I had not yet come to the last word, 私が最後の言葉に至らぬうちに、
80 che del suo mezzo fece il lume centro, when the light made its middle its center, その光は自らを中心とし、
81 girando sé come veloce mola; spinning on itself like a swift millstone; 速い石臼のように回転した。
82 poi rispuose l’amor che v’era dentro: then the love that was within it answered: そして、その内にあった愛が答えた。
83 «Luce divina sopra me s’appunta, «Divine light is focused upon me, 「神の光が私の上に注がれ、
84 penetrando per questa in ch’io m’inventro, penetrating through this in which I in-womb myself, 我が胎内となるこの光を貫き、
85 la cui virtù, col mio veder congiunta, whose power, joined with my vision, その徳は、私の視覚と結びついて、
86 mi leva sopra me tanto, ch’i’ veggio raises me so far above myself, that I see 私を自らの上へと高く引き上げ、それゆえ私は見る
87 la somma essenza de la quale è munta. the supreme essence from which it is milked. その搾り出される至高の本質を。
88 Quinci vien l’allegrezza ond’ io fiammeggio; From this comes the joy with which I flame; これゆえに、私が燃え輝く喜びは来る。
89 per ch’a la vista mia, quant’ ella è chiara, because to my sight, as much as it is clear, なぜなら、私の視界の清澄さに応じて、
90 la chiarità de la fiamma pareggio. I match the clarity of the flame. 私は炎の輝きを等しくするからだ。
91 Ma quell’ alma nel ciel che più si schiara, But that soul in heaven that is most illumined, だが、天国で最も輝くあの魂も、
92 quel serafin che ’n Dio più l’occhio ha fisso, that seraph who has his eye most fixed on God, 神に最も眼差しを据えるあの熾天使も、
93 a la dimanda tua non satisfara, would not satisfy your demand, お前の問いに満足な答えはできぬだろう、
94 però che sì s’innoltra ne lo abisso because what you ask so enters the abyss なぜなら、お前が問うことは、あまりに深く分け入るからだ
95 de l’etterno statuto quel che chiedi, of the eternal statute 永遠の定めの深淵へと、
96 che da ogne creata vista è scisso. that it is cut off from every created sight. いかなる被造物の視界からも断ち切られているのだ。
97 E al mondo mortal, quando tu riedi, And to the mortal world, when you return, そして、お前が定命の世界へ戻ったなら、
98 questo rapporta, sì che non presumma report this, so that it may not presume これを伝えよ、そうすれば思い上がって
99 a tanto segno più mover li piedi. to move its feet toward such a mark again. もはやそのような印へと歩を進めることはないだろう。
100 La mente, che qui luce, in terra fumma; The mind, which here is light, on earth is smoke; ここで光る知性も、地上では煙だ。
101 onde riguarda come può là giùe so consider how it can do there below ゆえに考えよ、いかにしてかの地で成し得ようか
102 quel che non pote perché ’l ciel l’assumma». what it cannot do even when heaven assumes it». 天がそれを取り上げても能わぬことを」。
103 Sì mi prescrisser le parole sue, His words so prescribed to me, 彼の言葉は私にそのように命じたので、
104 ch’io lasciai la quistione e mi ritrassi that I left the question and drew back 私はその問いを捨てて身を引き、
105 a dimandarla umilmente chi fue. to ask him humbly who he was. 謙虚に彼が誰であったかを尋ねた。
106 «Tra ’ due liti d’Italia surgon sassi, «Between the two shores of Italy rise rocks, 「イタリアの二つの岸の間に岩山がそびえ、
107 e non molto distanti a la tua patria, and not very distant from your homeland, そなたの故郷からさほど遠くない、
108 tanto che ’ troni assai suonan più bassi, so that thunders sound far lower, 雷鳴もはるか下に響くほど高く、
109 e fanno un gibbo che si chiama Catria, and they make a hump that is called Catria, カトリアと呼ばれる一つの峰をなしている。
110 di sotto al quale è consecrato un ermo, beneath which is consecrated a hermitage, その麓には聖なる庵が捧げられており、
111 che suole esser disposto a sola latria». which used to be disposed to worship alone». かつてはただ神への礼拝にのみ専念していた」。
112 Così ricominciommi il terzo sermo; Thus the third speech began to me again; このようにして三度目の言葉が私に始まり、
113 e poi, continüando, disse: «Quivi and then, continuing, he said: «There そして、続けて言った、「そこで私は
114 al servigio di Dio mi fe’ sì fermo, in the service of God I made myself so firm, 神への奉仕に身を固め、
115 che pur con cibi di liquor d’ulivi that just with foods seasoned with olive liquor ただオリーブの汁で味付けた食物だけで
116 lievemente passava caldi e geli, I lightly passed through heats and frosts, 暑さも寒さも軽やかに過ごし、
117 contento ne’ pensier contemplativi. content in contemplative thoughts. 観想の思いに満ち足りていた。
118 Render solea quel chiostro a questi cieli That cloister used to render to these heavens かの修道院はかつてこれらの天に
119 fertilemente; e ora è fatto vano, fruitfully; and now it is made so vain, 豊かに実りをもたらした。だが今は虚しく、
120 sì che tosto convien che si riveli. that soon it must be revealed. やがてそのことが露わにならねばなるまい。
121 In quel loco fu’ io Pietro Damiano, In that place I was Peter Damian, その場所にいた私はピエトロ・ダミアーノ、
122 e Pietro Peccator fu’ ne la casa and Peter the Sinner I was in the house そしてピエトロ・ペッカトール（罪人ピエトロ）であった、アドリア海の岸辺にある
123 di Nostra Donna in sul lito adriano. of Our Lady on the Adriatic shore. 我らの貴婦人（聖母マリア）の館で。
124 Poca vita mortal m’era rimasa, Little mortal life was left to me, 私の死すべき命はわずかしか残っていなかった、
125 quando fui chiesto e tratto a quel cappello, when I was sought and drawn to that hat, 私が求められ、かの帽子へと引き立てられた時、
126 che pur di male in peggio si travasa. which is ever poured from bad to worse. それはただ悪からさらに悪へと移りゆくものだが。
127 Venne Cefàs e venne il gran vasello Cephas came, and the great vessel came ケファスも来た、聖霊の大いなる器も来た、
128 de lo Spirito Santo, magri e scalzi, of the Holy Spirit, thin and barefoot, 痩せて裸足で、
129 prendendo il cibo da qualunque ostello. taking their food from any inn. いかなる宿からも食物を得ながら。
130 Or voglion quinci e quindi chi rincalzi Now modern pastors want those on this side and that 今の牧者たちは、こちらからもあちらからも支える者を、
131 li moderni pastori e chi li meni, to prop them up and to lead them, そして導く者を欲し、あまりに重いので、
132 tanto son gravi, e chi di rietro li alzi. so heavy are they, and one to hoist them from behind. 後ろから持ち上げてくれる者さえも欲する。
133 Cuopron d’i manti loro i palafreni, They cover their palfreys with their mantles, 彼らはその外套で馬を覆い、
134 sì che due bestie van sott’ una pelle: so that two beasts go under a single skin: そのため二つの獣が一つの皮の下を行く。
135 oh pazïenza che tanto sostieni!». O patience that endures so much!». おお、かくも耐え忍ぶ忍耐よ！」。
136 A questa voce vid’ io più fiammelle At this voice I saw more little flames この声に、私はさらに多くの炎が
137 di grado in grado scendere e girarsi, descend from step to step and wheel around, 段から段へと下りてきては旋回し、
138 e ogne giro le facea più belle. and every turn made them more beautiful. 一巡りごとに、ますます美しくなるのを見た。
139 Dintorno a questa vennero e fermarsi, They came around this one and stopped, この魂の周りに集まって立ち止まり、
140 e fero un grido di sì alto suono, and made a cry of so high a sound, そして、かくも高らかな叫び声を上げた、
141 che non potrebbe qui assomigliarsi; that here it could not be resembled; この世では比べようもないほどの。
142 né io lo ’ntesi, sì mi vinse il tuono. nor did I understand it, the thunder so overcame me. その雷鳴に圧倒され、私には理解できなかった。
