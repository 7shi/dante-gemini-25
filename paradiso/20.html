Paradiso — Canto 20

L'Aquila identifica i sei sovrani giusti che formano il suo occhio e, rispondendo a un dubbio di Dante, spiega il mistero della salvezza dei pagani Traiano e Rifeo attraverso l'imperscrutabile grazia divina e la predestinazione. The Eagle identifies the six just rulers forming its eye and, responding to Dante's doubt, explains the mystery of the salvation of the pagans Trajan and Ripheus through inscrutable divine grace and predestination. 鷲はその眼を形作る六人の正義の君主を明かし、ダンテの疑念に応えて、異教徒トラヤヌスとリフェウスの救済の奥義を、計り知れない神の恩寵と予定を通して説明する。

1 Quando colui che tutto ’l mondo alluma When he who lights all the world 全世界を照らす者が
2 de l’emisperio nostro sì discende, descends so far from our hemisphere 我らの半球からかくも下り、
3 che ’l giorno d’ogne parte si consuma, that the day on every side is consumed, いかなる場所でも昼が尽きるとき、
4 lo ciel, che sol di lui prima s’accende, the sky, which is kindled by him alone before, 空は、初めは彼によってのみ輝き、
5 subitamente si rifà parvente suddenly makes itself visible again 突如として再び姿を現す
6 per molte luci, in che una risplende; through many lights, in which one light shines; その中で一つの光が輝く多くの光によって。
7 e questo atto del ciel mi venne a mente, and this act of the heavens came to my mind そしてこの天の行いが私の心に浮かんだ、
8 come ’l segno del mondo e de’ suoi duci when the sign of the world and of its leaders 世界とその指導者たちの印が
9 nel benedetto rostro fu tacente; in its blessed beak fell silent; 祝福された嘴の中で沈黙したとき。
10 però che tutte quelle vive luci, because all of those living lights, なぜなら、それら全ての生ける光は、
11 vie più lucendo, cominciaron canti shining far more brightly, began songs はるかに輝きを増し、歌い始めたからだ
12 da mia memoria labili e caduci. that from my memory are fleeting and have fallen. 私の記憶から滑り落ち、消えゆく歌を。
13 O dolce amor che di riso t’ammanti, O sweet love that cloaks yourself in a smile, おお、微笑みを衣とする甘美な愛よ、
14 quanto parevi ardente in que’ flailli, how ardent you appeared in those flares あの笛の音の中で、汝はいかに燃えているように見えたことか、
15 ch’avieno spirto sol di pensier santi! which had breath only of holy thoughts! それらは聖なる思いのみを息吹としていた！
16 Poscia che i cari e lucidi lapilli After the dear and lucid gems 愛しく輝く宝石たちが、
17 ond’ io vidi ingemmato il sesto lume with which I saw the sixth light bejeweled それらによって私が第六の光が飾られるのを見た
18 puoser silenzio a li angelici squilli, put silence to their angelic chimes, 天使の歌声に沈黙を置いた後、
19 udir mi parve un mormorar di fiume I seemed to hear the murmur of a river 川のせせらぎが聞こえるようだった
20 che scende chiaro giù di pietra in pietra, that flows down clear from rock to rock, 岩から岩へと清らかに下り、
21 mostrando l’ubertà del suo cacume. showing the abundance of its mountain crest. その頂の豊かさを示しながら。
22 E come suono al collo de la cetra And as sound at the neck of the lute そしてツィターの首で音が
23 prende sua forma, e sì com’ al pertugio takes its form, and as at the opening その形をとるように、また、穴で
24 de la sampogna vento che penètra, of the bagpipe the wind that enters it, サンポーニャに吹き込む風がそうであるように、
25 così, rimosso d’aspettare indugio, so, with no delay of waiting removed, かくして、待つという遅れを取り除き、
26 quel mormorar de l’aguglia salissi that murmur of the eagle rose up その鷲のせせらぎは昇っていった
27 su per lo collo, come fosse bugio. through its neck, as if it were hollow. その首を、まるで空洞であるかのように。
28 Fecesi voce quivi, e quindi uscissi There it became voice, and from there it issued そこで声となり、そしてそこから出た
29 per lo suo becco in forma di parole, through its beak in the form of words, その嘴から、言葉の形で、
30 quali aspettava il core ov’ io le scrissi. such as the heart awaited where I wrote them. 私が心に書き留めたその心が待ち望んでいた言葉の。
31 «La parte in me che vede e pate il sole “The part in me that sees and endures the sun 「地上の鷲において、太陽を見、耐える我が部分を」
32 ne l’aguglie mortali», incominciommi, in mortal eagles,” it began to me, とそれは私に語り始めた、
33 «or fisamente riguardar si vole, “now wishes to be regarded intently, 「今や、しっかりと見つめるがよい、
34 perché d’i fuochi ond’ io figura fommi, for of the fires from which I form my figure, なぜなら、私が姿を形作る炎のうち、
35 quelli onde l’occhio in testa mi scintilla, those from which the eye in my head sparkles, 頭の眼で私を輝かせるそれらは、
36 e’ di tutti lor gradi son li sommi. are the highest of all their ranks. その全ての階級の中でも最高位にあるからだ。
37 Colui che luce in mezzo per pupilla, He who shines in the middle as the pupil, 瞳孔として中央で輝く者は、
38 fu il cantor de lo Spirito Santo, was the singer of the Holy Spirit, 聖霊の歌い手であった、
39 che l’arca traslatò di villa in villa: who transported the ark from town to town: 契約の箱を町から町へと移した者だ。
40 ora conosce il merto del suo canto, now he knows the merit of his song, 今や彼は自らの歌の功績を知る、
41 in quanto effetto fu del suo consiglio, inasmuch as it was the effect of his own counsel, それが自らの意志の結果であった分だけ、
42 per lo remunerar ch’è altrettanto. by the reward which is its equal. それに見合う報いによって。
43 Dei cinque che mi fan cerchio per ciglio, Of the five who make a circle for my eyelid, 私の眉として円をなす五人のうち、
44 colui che più al becco mi s’accosta, he who is closest to my beak, 最も嘴に近づく者は、
45 la vedovella consolò del figlio: consoled the little widow for her son: 寡婦をその息子のことで慰めた。
46 ora conosce quanto caro costa now he knows how dearly it costs 今や彼は、いかに高くつくかを知る
47 non seguir Cristo, per l’esperïenza not to follow Christ, by the experience キリストに従わぬことが、その経験によって
48 di questa dolce vita e de l’opposta. of this sweet life and of the opposite. この甘美な生と、その反対の生の。
49 E quel che segue in la circunferenza And he who follows in the circumference そして、私が語る円周の
50 di che ragiono, per l’arco superno, of which I speak, on the upper arc, 上の弧で、次に続く者は、
51 morte indugiò per vera penitenza: delayed death through true penitence: 真の悔い改めによって死を遅らせた。
52 ora conosce che ’l giudicio etterno now he knows that the eternal judgment 今や彼は、永遠の裁きは
53 non si trasmuta, quando degno preco is not changed, when a worthy prayer 変わらないことを知る、価値ある祈りが
54 fa crastino là giù de l’odïerno. down there makes today’s fate into tomorrow’s. 地上で今日のものを明日のものにするときも。
55 L’altro che segue, con le leggi e meco, The other who follows, with the laws and me, 次に続く者は、法と私と共に、
56 sotto buona intenzion che fé mal frutto, under good intention that bore evil fruit, 悪しき実を結んだ善き意図のもと、
57 per cedere al pastor si fece greco: to give way to the shepherd, made himself a Greek: 牧者に譲るために、自らをギリシャ人とした。
58 ora conosce come il mal dedutto now he knows how the evil deduced 今や彼は、いかに悪が
59 dal suo bene operar non li è nocivo, from his good action is not harmful to him, 自らの善行から生じても彼を害さないかを知る、
60 avvegna che sia ’l mondo indi distrutto. although the world is destroyed by it. それによって世界が滅びたとしても。
61 E quel che vedi ne l’arco declivo, And he whom you see in the downward sloping arc, そして下り坂の弧に見える者は、
62 Guiglielmo fu, cui quella terra plora was William, for whom that land weeps グリエルモであった。その地は彼を嘆き
63 che piagne Carlo e Federigo vivo: that mourns for a living Charles and Frederick: 生きているシャルルとフェデリーコを嘆いている。
64 ora conosce come s’innamora now he knows how heaven falls in love 今や彼は、いかに恋するかを知る
65 lo ciel del giusto rege, e al sembiante with a just king, and in the semblance 天が正しき王に、そしてその姿によって
66 del suo fulgore il fa vedere ancora. of his radiance he still makes it visible. その輝きの姿を今も見せている。
67 Chi crederebbe giù nel mondo errante Who down in the erring world would believe 迷える下界で誰が信じようか
68 che Rifëo Troiano in questo tondo that Ripheus the Trojan in this round circle この円の中にトロイア人リフェウスが
69 fosse la quinta de le luci sante? was the fifth of the holy lights? 第五の聖なる光であることを？
70 Ora conosce assai di quel che ’l mondo Now he knows much of that which the world 今や彼は、世界が
71 veder non può de la divina grazia, cannot see of divine grace, 神の恩寵について見ることのできない多くを知る、
72 ben che sua vista non discerna il fondo». although his sight does not discern the bottom.” その視力は底を見極めることはできないが」。
73 Quale allodetta che ’n aere si spazia Like a lark that soars in the air, 空に舞う雲雀が
74 prima cantando, e poi tace contenta first singing, and then falls silent, content まず歌い、そして満ち足りて黙るように
75 de l’ultima dolcezza che la sazia, with the final sweetness that sates it, 自らを飽かす最後の甘美さに、
76 tal mi sembiò l’imago de la ’mprenta so seemed to me the image of the imprint そのように私には思われた、永遠の喜びの
77 de l’etterno piacere, al cui disio of the eternal pleasure, by whose desire 印であるその姿は、その喜びの望みのままに
78 ciascuna cosa qual ell’ è diventa. each thing becomes what it is. すべてのものはあるがままの姿となるのだ。
79 E avvegna ch’io fossi al dubbiar mio And although I was, in my doubting, そして、私の抱く疑問に対して私は
80 lì quasi vetro a lo color ch’el veste, there almost like glass to the color that clothes it, そこでは、それを覆う色に対するガラスのようであったが、
81 tempo aspettar tacendo non patio, I could not bear to wait a time in silence, 黙して時を待つことには耐えられず、
82 ma de la bocca, «Che cose son queste?», but from my mouth, “What things are these?”, 口からは「これらは何という事なのか？」という言葉が
83 mi pinse con la forza del suo peso: was pushed by the force of its own weight: その重みの力で押し出された。
84 per ch’io di coruscar vidi gran feste. for which I saw great festivals of sparkling light. それゆえに私は、大いなる祝祭が閃くのを見た。
85 Poi appresso, con l’occhio più acceso, Then afterward, with its eye more kindled, すると次に、より燃えるような眼差しで、
86 lo benedetto segno mi rispuose the blessed sign answered me その祝福された印は私に答えた、
87 per non tenermi in ammirar sospeso: so as not to keep me suspended in wonder: 私を驚嘆のうちに宙吊りにしておかないために。
88 «Io veggio che tu credi queste cose “I see that you believe these things 「私はお前がこれらの事を信じているのを見る、
89 perch’ io le dico, ma non vedi come; because I say them, but you do not see how; 私がそれを語るゆえに。だが、その理（ことわり）は見えていない。
90 sì che, se son credute, sono ascose. so that, if they are believed, they are hidden. それゆえ、信じられてはいても、隠されているのだ。
91 Fai come quei che la cosa per nome You do as one who the thing by name お前は、ある事物を名によって
92 apprende ben, ma la sua quiditate grasps well, but its quiddity よく知ってはいるが、その本質を
93 veder non può se altri non la prome. cannot see if another does not bring it forth. 他の者が示さねば見ることのできぬ者のようだ。
94 Regnum celorum vïolenza pate The kingdom of heaven suffers violence 天の国は猛りを受けるのだ、
95 da caldo amore e da viva speranza, from warm love and from living hope, 熱き愛と生ける希望によって。
96 che vince la divina volontate: which conquers the divine will: それは神の御心を打ち破る。
97 non a guisa che l’omo a l’om sobranza, not in the way that man overcomes man, 人が人を打ち負かす仕方ではなく、
98 ma vince lei perché vuole esser vinta, but it conquers it because it wants to be conquered, 神が打ち破られることを望むゆえに打ち破り、
99 e, vinta, vince con sua beninanza. and, conquered, it conquers with its own benevolence. そして、打ち破られて、その慈悲をもって打ち破るのだ。
100 La prima vita del ciglio e la quinta The first life of the eyebrow and the fifth 眉をなす第一の魂と第五の魂は
101 ti fa maravigliar, perché ne vedi make you marvel, because in them you see お前を驚かせる。なぜなら、お前は彼らのうちに
102 la regïon de li angeli dipinta. the region of the angels painted. 天使たちの領域が描かれているのを見るからだ。
103 D’i corpi suoi non uscir, come credi, They did not leave their bodies, as you believe, その肉体から離れたのではない、そなたが思うように、
104 Gentili, ma Cristiani, in ferma fede Pagan, but Christian, in firm faith, 異教徒としてではなく、キリスト教徒として、固い信仰のうちに、
105 quel d’i passuri e quel d’i passi piedi. the one in the feet to suffer, the other in the feet which had suffered. 一人は来たるべき御足を、もう一人は過ぎ去りし御足を信じたのだ。
106 Ché l’una de lo ’nferno, u’ non si riede For one of them from Hell, whence one never returns というのも、一方は地獄から、そこは善き意志をもって
107 già mai a buon voler, tornò a l’ossa; with a good will, came back to his bones; 決して戻ることのない場所だが、骨へと戻ったのだ。
108 e ciò di viva spene fu mercede: and this was the reward of living hope: そしてそれは、生きた希望への報いであった。
109 di viva spene, che mise la possa of living hope, which put its power 生きた希望、それは力を込めた
110 ne’ prieghi fatti a Dio per suscitarla, into the prayers made to God to resurrect him, 彼を蘇らせるために神へ捧げられた祈りの中に、
111 sì che potesse sua voglia esser mossa. so that his will might be moved. その意志が動かされることができるようにと。
112 L’anima glorïosa onde si parla, The glorious soul of whom is spoken, 語られているその栄光ある魂は、
113 tornata ne la carne, in che fu poco, returned to the flesh, in which he was for little time, 肉体に戻り、そこにはわずかな間しかいなかったが、
114 credette in lui che potëa aiutarla; believed in Him who had the power to help him; 自らを助けることのできる御方を信じた。
115 e credendo s’accese in tanto foco and believing, was kindled with so great a fire そして信じることで、それほどの炎に燃え上がったのだ、
116 di vero amor, ch’a la morte seconda of true love, that at his second death 真の愛の、第二の死において
117 fu degna di venire a questo gioco. he was worthy to come to this joyful game. この遊戯（天国）に来るにふさわしくなったのだ。
118 L’altra, per grazia che da sì profonda The other, through grace that from so deep もう一方は、それほど深い泉から
119 fontana stilla, che mai creatura a fountain drips, that never has a creature 滴り落ちる恩寵によって、いかなる被造物も
120 non pinse l’occhio infino a la prima onda, pushed its eye as far as the first wave, その最初の波まで目を凝らしたことのない、
121 tutto suo amor là giù pose a drittura: placed all his love down there on righteousness: その愛のすべてを、地上で正義に向けた。
122 per che, di grazia in grazia, Dio li aperse for which, from grace to grace, God opened それゆえ、恩寵から恩寵へと、神は彼の目を開かれた
123 l’occhio a la nostra redenzion futura; his eye to our future redemption; 我らの未来の贖罪へと。
124 ond’ ei credette in quella, e non sofferse wherefore he believed in it, and from then on そこで彼はそれを信じ、もはや耐えられなくなったのだ、
125 da indi il puzzo più del paganesmo; endured no more the stench of paganism; その時から、異教の悪臭を。
126 e riprendiene le genti perverse. and he reproached the perverse peoples. そして邪悪な民を非難したのだ。
127 Quelle tre donne li fur per battesmo Those three ladies whom you saw at the right wheel あの三人の貴婦人が、彼にとって洗礼となった、
128 che tu vedesti da la destra rota, were his for baptism, そなたが右の車輪で見た、
129 dinanzi al battezzar più d’un millesmo. more than a thousand years before baptizing was. 洗礼の儀式より千年もの昔に。
130 O predestinazion, quanto remota O predestination, how remote おお、予定説よ、なんと遠いことか
131 è la radice tua da quelli aspetti is your root from those sights そなたの根源は、あの視線からは、
132 che la prima cagion non veggion tota! that do not see the First Cause in its entirety! 第一原因を完全には見ない者たちの！
133 E voi, mortali, tenetevi stretti And you mortals, hold yourselves back そして汝ら、死すべき者よ、慎むがよい
134 a giudicar: ché noi, che Dio vedemo, in judging: for we, who see God, 裁くことを。なぜなら我ら、神を見る者でさえ、
135 non conosciamo ancor tutti li eletti; do not yet know all the elect; 選ばれし者をすべて知っているわけではないのだから。
136 ed ènne dolce così fatto scemo, and such a lack is sweet to us, そして我らには甘美なのだ、そのように欠けていることが。
137 perché il ben nostro in questo ben s’affina, because our good is perfected in this good, なぜなら我らの善はこの善の中で磨かれ、
138 che quel che vole Iddio, e noi volemo». that what God wills, we also will.” 神が望むことを、我らも望むからだ」。
139 Così da quella imagine divina, Thus by that divine image, このようにして、あの神々しい姿から、
140 per farmi chiara la mia corta vista, to make my short sight clear, 私の短い視界を明らかにするために、
141 data mi fu soave medicina. was a sweet medicine given to me. 甘美な薬が私に与えられた。
142 E come a buon cantor buon citarista And as to a good singer a good citharist そして、良き歌い手に良き琴弾きが
143 fa seguitar lo guizzo de la corda, makes the quiver of the string follow, 弦の震えを追わせるように、
144 in che più di piacer lo canto acquista, by which the song acquires more pleasure, それによって歌はより多くの喜びを得るのだが、
145 sì, mentre ch’e’ parlò, sì mi ricorda so, while it spoke, I remember そう、それが語る間、私は思い出す、
146 ch’io vidi le due luci benedette, that I saw the two blessed lights, あの二つの祝福された光が、
147 pur come batter d’occhi si concorda, just as the blinking of eyes agrees, まるで瞬きが一致するように、
148 con le parole mover le fiammette. move their little flames with the words. 言葉と共にその小さな炎を動かすのを。
