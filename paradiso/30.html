Paradiso — Canto 30

Dante, abbagliato e purificato nell’Empireo, contempla con Beatrice la rosa celeste e le due corti del cielo, mentre ella gli mostra il seggio di Arrigo e profetizza la corruzione papale. Dante, dazzled and purified in the Empyrean, contemplates with Beatrice the celestial rose and the two courts of Heaven, while she shows him Henry’s seat and prophesies papal corruption. ダンテは至高天で眩惑され浄められ、ベアトリーチェとともに天上の薔薇と天国の二つの宮廷を観想する一方、彼女はアッリーゴの座を示し、教皇の腐敗を予言する。

1 Forse semilia miglia di lontano Perhaps six thousand miles away おそらく六千マイルの彼方では
2 ci ferve l’ora sesta, e questo mondo the sixth hour grows hot for us, and this world 我らに第六の時が燃え、この世界は
3 china già l’ombra quasi al letto piano, already inclines its shadow almost to the level bed, すでに影をほとんど平らな床へと傾けている、
4 quando ’l mezzo del cielo, a noi profondo, when the middle of the sky, deep to us, その時、我らにとって深淵なる天の中ほどは
5 comincia a farsi tal, ch’alcuna stella begins to become such, that some star そのようなものになり始め、いかなる星も
6 perde il parere infino a questo fondo; loses its appearance down to this bottom; この底に至るまでその姿を失う。
7 e come vien la chiarissima ancella and as the brightest handmaiden comes そして太陽の最も輝かしい侍女が
8 del sol più oltre, così ’l ciel si chiude of the sun further on, so the sky closes さらに進み来ると、そのように天は閉ざされる、
9 di vista in vista infino a la più bella. from sight to sight, even to the most beautiful. 次々と視界から、最も美しいものに至るまで。
10 Non altrimenti il trïunfo che lude Not otherwise the triumph that plays それと異ならず、私を打ち負かした点を
11 sempre dintorno al punto che mi vinse, always around the point that defeated me, 常にその周りで戯れる凱旋は、
12 parendo inchiuso da quel ch’elli ’nchiude, seeming enclosed by that which it encloses, それを包むものによって包まれているかのように見えながら、
13 a poco a poco al mio veder si stinse: little by little from my sight was extinguished: 次第に私の視界から消え去っていった。
14 per che tornar con li occhi a Bëatrice wherefore to turn my eyes to Beatrice それゆえにベアトリーチェに眼を戻すことを
15 nulla vedere e amor mi costrinse. seeing nothing else and love constrained me. 何も見えぬことと愛が私に強いた。
16 Se quanto infino a qui di lei si dice If all that up to now has been said of her もしこれまで彼女について語られてきたことすべてが
17 fosse conchiuso tutto in una loda, were all contained in a single praise, 一つの賛辞の中にすべてまとめられたとしても、
18 poca sarebbe a fornir questa vice. it would be little to serve for this occasion. この務めを果たすには僅かであろう。
19 La bellezza ch’io vidi si trasmoda The beauty that I saw so transcends 私が見た美しさは人間性を超えている、
20 non pur di là da noi, ma certo io credo not only beyond us, but surely I believe 我々の彼方にあるだけでなく、確かに私は信じる、
21 che solo il suo fattor tutta la goda. that only its maker enjoys it all. ただその創り主のみがそれをすべて享受することを。
22 Da questo passo vinto mi concedo By this passage I concede myself defeated この詩句において私は自ら敗北を認める、
23 più che già mai da punto di suo tema more than ever by a point of his theme かつていかなる主題の点によっても
24 soprato fosse comico o tragedo: was a comic or tragic poet overcome: 喜劇詩人や悲劇詩人が打ち負かされた以上に。
25 ché, come sole in viso che più trema, for, like the sun on a face that most trembles, なぜなら、最も震える眼差しの中の太陽のように、
26 così lo rimembrar del dolce riso so the memory of the sweet smile そのように、あの甘い微笑みの記憶は
27 la mente mia da me medesmo scema. diminishes my mind from my very self. 私の精神を私自身から奪い去る。
28 Dal primo giorno ch’i’ vidi il suo viso From the first day that I saw her face 私がこの世で彼女の顔を見た最初の日から、
29 in questa vita, infino a questa vista, in this life, up to this sight, この光景に至るまで、
30 non m’è il seguire al mio cantar preciso; the following in my song has not been cut off; 私の歌における追跡は途絶えたことはなかった。
31 ma or convien che mio seguir desista but now it is fitting that my pursuit desist しかし今こそ私の追跡は諦めねばならない、
32 più dietro a sua bellezza, poetando, further behind her beauty, in my poetizing, 詩作において、さらに彼女の美の後を追うことを、
33 come a l’ultimo suo ciascuno artista. as every artist at his ultimate limit. あらゆる芸術家がその極致においてそうするように。
34 Cotal qual io lascio a maggior bando Such as it is, I leave to a greater heralding このような様を、私はより大いなる告知手に委ねる
35 che quel de la mia tuba, che deduce than that of my trumpet, which brings 私のラッパのそれよりも、それは導き出すのだ
36 l’ardüa sua matera terminando, its arduous matter to a close, その困難な主題を、終わりへと。
37 con atto e voce di spedito duce with the act and voice of a swift leader 迅速な導き手の身のこなしと声で
38 ricominciò: «Noi siamo usciti fore she began again: “We have come forth 彼女は再び語り始めた。「我らは外に出ました
39 del maggior corpo al ciel ch’è pura luce: from the greatest body to the heaven that is pure light: 最大の天体から、純粋な光である天へと。
40 luce intellettüal, piena d’amore; intellectual light, full of love; 知的な光、愛に満ちた光。
41 amor di vero ben, pien di letizia; love of true good, full of joy; 真の善への愛、喜びに満ちた愛。
42 letizia che trascende ogne dolzore. joy that transcends all sweetness. あらゆる甘美さを超越する喜び。
43 Qui vederai l’una e l’altra milizia Here you will see the one and the other militia ここであなたは一方ともう一方の軍勢を見るでしょう
44 di paradiso, e l’una in quelli aspetti of paradise, and the one in those aspects 天国の、そして一方はあの姿で
45 che tu vedrai a l’ultima giustizia». that you will see at the last judgment.” あなたが最後の審判の時に見るであろう姿で」。
46 Come sùbito lampo che discetti Like a sudden flash of lightning that scatters 突然の稲妻が、霧散させるように
47 li spiriti visivi, sì che priva the visual spirits, so that it deprives 視覚の霊を、その結果、奪い去るように
48 da l’atto l’occhio di più forti obietti, the eye of the act of seeing stronger objects, より強い対象を見るという行為から目を、
49 così mi circunfulse luce viva, so a living light shone all around me, そのように生き生きとした光が私を包み込み、
50 e lasciommi fasciato di tal velo and left me swathed in such a veil そして私をこのようなヴェールで包んだままにした
51 del suo fulgor, che nulla m’appariva. of its splendor, that nothing appeared to me. その輝きの、何も私には見えなかった。
52 «Sempre l’amor che queta questo cielo “Always the love that pacifies this heaven 「常にこの天を静める愛は
53 accoglie in sé con sì fatta salute, welcomes into itself with such a greeting, このような挨拶をもって自らの内に迎えるのです、
54 per far disposto a sua fiamma il candelo». to make the candle ready for its flame.” その炎に対して蝋燭を備えさせるために」。
55 Non fur più tosto dentro a me venute No sooner had these brief words この短い言葉が私の内に
56 queste parole brievi, ch’io compresi come into me, than I understood 入ってくるやいなや、私は理解した
57 me sormontar di sopr’ a mia virtute; myself to be rising above my own power; 私が自らの能力を超えて昇るのを。
58 e di novella vista mi raccesi and I was rekindled with such new sight そして私は新たな視力に再び燃え上がり、
59 tale, che nulla luce è tanto mera, that no light is so pure それはいかなる光もそれほど純粋ではなく、
60 che li occhi miei non si fosser difesi; that my eyes could not have withstood it; 私の目がそれに耐えられなかったであろうほどだった。
61 e vidi lume in forma di rivera and I saw a light in the form of a river, そして私は川の形をした光を見た
62 fulvido di fulgore, intra due rive tawny with splendor, between two banks 輝きに満ちて赤みがかった、二つの岸の間に
63 dipinte di mirabil primavera. painted with a miraculous spring. 驚くべき春の景色で彩られた。
64 Di tal fiumana uscian faville vive, From this great river came forth living sparks, そのような大河から生きた火花が流れ出て、
65 e d’ogne parte si mettien ne’ fiori, and from every side they set themselves in the flowers, そしてあらゆる方角で花々の中に身を置いた、
66 quasi rubin che oro circunscrive; almost like rubies that gold encircles; まるで金が縁取るルビーのように。
67 poi, come inebrïate da li odori, then, as if inebriated by the scents, それから、その香りに酔いしれたかのように、
68 riprofondavan sé nel miro gurge, they plunged themselves again into the wondrous torrent, 驚くべき渦の中へと再び身を沈め、
69 e s’una intrava, un’altra n’uscia fori. and if one entered, another came forth from it. そして一つが入ると、もう一つが外へ出てきた。
70 «L’alto disio che mo t’infiamma e urge, “The high desire that now inflames and urges you, 「今あなたを燃え立たせ、駆り立てるその高き欲望は、
71 d’aver notizia di ciò che tu vei, to have knowledge of that which you see, あなたが見るものについての知識を得ようとする、
72 tanto mi piace più quanto più turge; pleases me the more, the more it swells; 膨らめば膨らむほど、私を一層喜ばせます。
73 ma di quest’ acqua convien che tu bei but of this water you must first drink しかし、この水をあなたは飲まねばなりません、
74 prima che tanta sete in te si sazi»: before so great a thirst in you is sated”: あなたの中のその大きな渇きが癒される前に」。
75 così mi disse il sol de li occhi miei. thus said to me the sun of my eyes. そう私の目の太陽は私に言った。
76 Anche soggiunse: «Il fiume e li topazi She also added: “The river and the topazes さらに付け加えた。「川と、トパーズ（のような火花）は、
77 ch’entrano ed escono e ’l rider de l’erbe that enter and exit and the smiling of the grasses 出入りし、そして草々の微笑みは、
78 son di lor vero umbriferi prefazi. are shadowy prefaces of their truth. それらの真の姿の、影を落とす序章なのです。
79 Non che da sé sian queste cose acerbe; Not that these things are immature in themselves; これらがそれ自体で未熟なわけではなく、
80 ma è difetto da la parte tua, but the defect is on your part, あなたの側に欠陥があるのです、
81 che non hai viste ancor tanto superbe». in that you do not yet have sight so exalted.” あなたはまだそれほど崇高な視力を持たないから」。
82 Non è fantin che sì sùbito rua There is no infant who so suddenly rushes これほど素早く突進する赤子はいまい、
83 col volto verso il latte, se si svegli with his face toward the milk, if he awakens 乳に向かって顔を、もし目覚めて、
84 molto tardato da l’usanza sua, much later than his custom, いつもの習慣よりずっと遅れたなら、
85 come fec’ io, per far migliori spegli as I did, to make better mirrors 私がしたように、我が目をなお一層良き鏡とするために、
86 ancor de li occhi, chinandomi a l’onda still of my eyes, bending down to the wave 身をかがめてその波に、
87 che si deriva perché vi s’immegli; that flows so that one may be bettered there; そこに浸ることで人がより良くなるために流れるその波に。
88 e sì come di lei bevve la gronda and as soon as the eaves of my eyelids そして、その水を庇が飲んだように、
89 de le palpebre mie, così mi parve drank of it, so it seemed to me 私のまぶたの庇が、私にはそう思われた、
90 di sua lunghezza divenuta tonda. that its length had become round. その長さが、円形になったと。
91 Poi, come gente stata sotto larve, Then, like people who have been under masks, それから、仮面の下にいた人々が、
92 che pare altro che prima, se si sveste who seem other than before, if they cast off 以前とは違う姿に見えるように、もし脱ぎ捨てるなら、
93 la sembianza non süa in che disparve, the semblance not their own in which they disappeared, 自らのものでない、その中に消えていた面影を、
94 così mi si cambiaro in maggior feste so into greater festival for me changed そのように私には、より大いなる祝祭へと変わった、
95 li fiori e le faville, sì ch’io vidi the flowers and the sparks, so that I saw 花々と火花は、その結果私は見た、
96 ambo le corti del ciel manifeste. both courts of heaven made manifest. 天の二つの宮廷が、明らかにされたのを。
97 O isplendor di Dio, per cu’ io vidi O splendor of God, through which I saw おお、神の輝きよ、それによって私は見た、
98 l’alto trïunfo del regno verace, the high triumph of the true kingdom, 真の王国の高き凱旋を、
99 dammi virtù a dir com’ ïo il vidi! give me the power to tell how I saw it! 私がそれを見たままに語る力を与えたまえ！
100 Lume è là sù che visibile face A light is there above that makes visible かしこには光があり、それは見えるようにする
101 lo creatore a quella creatura the creator to that creature 創造主を、かの被造物に対して
102 che solo in lui vedere ha la sua pace. who only in seeing him has its peace. その被造物はただ主を見ることの中に自らの平安を見出す。
103 E’ si distende in circular figura, And it extends in a circular figure, そしてそれは円い姿に広がり、
104 in tanto che la sua circunferenza to such a degree that its circumference その円周たるや、あまりに広大で
105 sarebbe al sol troppo larga cintura. would be for the sun too wide a belt. 太陽にとってすらあまりに広すぎる帯となるであろう。
106 Fassi di raggio tutta sua parvenza Its entire appearance is made of a ray その姿のすべては一条の光線から成り、
107 reflesso al sommo del mobile primo, reflected from the summit of the first mobile, 原動天の頂に反射し、
108 che prende quindi vivere e potenza. which from there takes its life and power. そこから生命と力を得ている。
109 E come clivo in acqua di suo imo And as a hillside in water at its base そして、麓の池水に丘の斜面が
110 si specchia, quasi per vedersi addorno, is mirrored, as if to see itself adorned, 自らの装いを眺めるかのように映り、
111 quando è nel verde e ne’ fioretti opimo, when it is rich in green and in little flowers, 緑と可憐な花々に満ちているとき、
112 sì, soprastando al lume intorno intorno, so, rising above the light all around, そのように、周りの光の上方にそびえ、
113 vidi specchiarsi in più di mille soglie I saw mirrored in more than a thousand tiers 私は千以上の階層に映るのを見た、
114 quanto di noi là sù fatto ha ritorno. all of our kind who have made their return up there. 我らの中からかしこへ帰還した者たちのすべてを。
115 E se l’infimo grado in sé raccoglie And if the lowest rank collects within itself そして、もし最下段の階層がそれほどの
116 sì grande lume, quanta è la larghezza so great a light, how great is the breadth 大いなる光を集めているのなら、どれほど広大であろうか
117 di questa rosa ne l’estreme foglie! of this rose in its outermost leaves! この薔薇の、最も外側の花びらに至るまでの広さは！
118 La vista mia ne l’ampio e ne l’altezza My sight in the breadth and in the height 私の視線はその広さと高さの中にあっても
119 non si smarriva, ma tutto prendeva was not lost, but took in all 迷うことなく、すべてを捉えていた
120 il quanto e ’l quale di quella allegrezza. the how much and the what kind of that joy. その歓喜がどれほどであり、どのようなものであるかを。
121 Presso e lontano, lì, né pon né leva: Near and far, there, neither adds nor takes away: かしこでは、近いも遠いも、意味をなさず、
122 ché dove Dio sanza mezzo governa, for where God without medium governs, というのも、神が媒介なしに統治される場所では、
123 la legge natural nulla rileva. the natural law has no relevance. 自然の法則は何の効力ももたないからである。
124 Nel giallo de la rosa sempiterna, Into the yellow of the sempiternal rose, 永遠の薔薇の黄色い中心で、
125 che si digrada e dilata e redole which slopes down and expands and breathes それは段をなし、広がり、そして香りを放つ、
126 odor di lode al sol che sempre verna, an odor of praise to the Sun that is ever vernal, 常に春なる太陽への賛美の香りを、
127 qual è colui che tace e dicer vole, like one who is silent and wishes to speak, 口を閉ざしつつも語りたいと願う者のように、
128 mi trasse Bëatrice, e disse: «Mira Beatrice drew me, and said: “Behold ベアトリーチェは私を引き寄せ、言った。「見なさい、
129 quanto è ’l convento de le bianche stole! how vast is the convent of the white robes! 白い衣をまとった者たちの集いが、いかに広大であるかを！
130 Vedi nostra città quant’ ella gira; See our city, how great its circuit; 我らの都が、どれほど大きな円を描いているか見なさい。
131 vedi li nostri scanni sì ripieni, see our thrones so filled, 我らの座が、いかに満たされているかを見なさい、
132 che poca gente più ci si disira. that few people are now awaited here. もはや、ここに望まれる者は僅かしかいないほどに。
133 E ’n quel gran seggio a che tu li occhi tieni And in that great throne on which you hold your eyes そして、そなたが目を向けているその大いなる玉座には、
134 per la corona che già v’è sù posta, because of the crown that is already placed above it, すでにその上に置かれている冠ゆえに、
135 prima che tu a queste nozze ceni, before you sup at this wedding feast, そなたがこの婚礼の宴に与る前に、
136 sederà l’alma, che fia giù agosta, will sit the soul, that on earth will be imperial, 地上において尊厳なる魂が座るだろう、
137 de l’alto Arrigo, ch’a drizzare Italia of the high Henry, who to set Italy straight 高貴なるハインリヒの、彼はイタリアを正すために
138 verrà in prima ch’ella sia disposta. will come before she is disposed to it. その準備が整う前にやって来るだろう。
139 La cieca cupidigia che v’ammalia The blind greed that bewitches you そなたらを惑わす盲目の貪欲が
140 simili fatti v’ha al fantolino has made you like the little infant そなたらを、あの赤子のようにしてしまった。
141 che muor per fame e caccia via la balia. who dies of hunger and drives away his nurse. 飢えで死に瀕しながら、乳母を追い払う赤子に。
142 E fia prefetto nel foro divino And a prefect in the divine forum will be そして、神の法廷の長官となるだろう、
143 allora tal, che palese e coverto then such a one, that openly and in secret その時、表向きにも内々にも、
144 non anderà con lui per un cammino. will not walk with him on a single path. 彼と同じ道を歩むことのない者が。
145 Ma poco poi sarà da Dio sofferto But not long then will he be suffered by God しかし神は、彼がその聖なる職にあるのを、
146 nel santo officio; ch’el sarà detruso in the holy office; for he shall be thrust down 長くはお許しにならないだろう。彼は突き落とされるのだ、
147 là dove Simon mago è per suo merto, there where Simon Magus is for his deserts, 魔術師シモンがその功績ゆえにいる場所へ、
148 e farà quel d’Alagna intrar più giuso». and will make the one from Alagna enter deeper.” そしてアナーニの者を、さらに深く押し込むだろう」。
