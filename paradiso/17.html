Paradiso — Canto 17

Dante's ancestor Cacciaguida foretells his painful exile from Florence but commands him to truthfully record his entire divine journey, assuring him it will bring lasting fame. 高祖父カッチャグイーダはダンテに追放という辛い未来を予言し、恐れずに見たもの全ての真実を語るという使命を託す。

1 Qual venne a Climenè, per accertarsi As he who came to Clymene, to make certain クリメネのもとへ、確かめるために来た、
2 di ciò ch’avëa incontro a sé udito, of that which he had heard against himself, 自分について聞いたことを、
3 quei ch’ancor fa li padri ai figli scarsi; he who still makes fathers to sons scarce; 今なお父を子に慎重にさせるあの者のように、
4 tal era io, e tal era sentito such was I, and such was I perceived そのような私であり、そのように感じ取られていた
5 e da Beatrice e da la santa lampa both by Beatrice and by the holy lamp ベアトリーチェにも、そして聖なる光にも、
6 che pria per me avea mutato sito. that had before for me changed its place. 先に私のために場所を移したその光にも。
7 Per che mia donna «Manda fuor la vampa Wherefore my lady “Send forth the flame それゆえ我が貴婦人は言った、「あなたの望みの
8 del tuo disio», mi disse, «sì ch’ella esca of your desire,” she said to me, “so that it may issue 炎を外へ放ちなさい。そうすればそれは現れるでしょう
9 segnata bene de la interna stampa: well-marked with the internal stamp: 内なる刻印がはっきりと押されて。
10 non perché nostra conoscenza cresca not so that our knowledge may grow あなたの言葉で我らの知識が増すためではなく、
11 per tuo parlare, ma perché t’ausi by your speaking, but so that you may dare あなたが自ら敢えて渇きを語り、
12 a dir la sete, sì che l’uom ti mesca». to state the thirst, so that one may pour for you.” 人があなたに注いでくれるようにするためです」。
13 «O cara piota mia che sì t’insusi, “O my dear root who so uplifts yourself, 「おお、我が親愛なる根源よ、あなたは高く昇り、
14 che, come veggion le terrene menti that, as earthly minds see 地上の知性が、三角形に二つの鈍角は
15 non capere in trïangol due ottusi, a triangle cannot contain two obtuse angles, 収まらないと見るように、
16 così vedi le cose contingenti so you see contingent things そのようにあなたは偶有の事柄を
17 anzi che sieno in sé, mirando il punto before they exist in themselves, gazing at the point それらが存在する前に見るのです、一点をみつめて、
18 a cui tutti li tempi son presenti; to which all times are present; そこには全ての時が現在としてあるのですから。
19 mentre ch’io era a Virgilio congiunto while I was joined with Virgil 私がウェルギリウスと共にあって
20 su per lo monte che l’anime cura up on the mountain that cures the souls 魂を浄める山を登り
21 e discendendo nel mondo defunto, and descending in the world of the dead, そして死者の世界を下っていた時、
22 dette mi fuor di mia vita futura were told to me of my future life 私の未来の人生について語られました
23 parole gravi, avvegna ch’io mi senta grave words, although I feel myself 重い言葉が。もっとも私は感じてはいますが
24 ben tetragono ai colpi di ventura; quite foursquare to the blows of fortune; 運命の打撃にはびくともしないと。
25 per che la voglia mia saria contenta for which my desire would be content それゆえ私の願いは満たされるでしょう
26 d’intender qual fortuna mi s’appressa: to understand what fortune draws near to me: どのような運命が私に近づくのかを理解することで。
27 ché saetta previsa vien più lenta». for a foreseen arrow comes more slowly.” なぜなら、予見された矢はより緩やかに来るのですから」。
28 Così diss’ io a quella luce stessa Thus said I to that same light かくして私は、あの光そのものに言った、
29 che pria m’avea parlato; e come volle that had spoken to me before; and as Beatrice 先に私に語りかけた光に。そしてベアトリーチェが
30 Beatrice, fu la mia voglia confessa. willed, my wish was confessed. 望んだように、私の願いは打ち明けられた。
31 Né per ambage, in che la gente folle Not with ambiguous words, in which the foolish people 愚かな民が、
32 già s’inviscava pria che fosse anciso once were entangled before was slain 罪を取り除く神の小羊が屠られる前に囚われていたような、
33 l’Agnel di Dio che le peccata tolle, the Lamb of God who takes away sins, 曖昧な言葉ではなく、
34 ma per chiare parole e con preciso but with clear words and with precise 明瞭な言葉と的確な
35 latin rispuose quello amor paterno, speech that paternal love replied, 話しぶりで、その父なる愛は答えた、
36 chiuso e parvente del suo proprio riso: enclosed and apparent in its own smile: 自らの微笑のうちに隠され、また現れながら。
37 «La contingenza, che fuor del quaderno "Contingency, which does not extend beyond the volume 「偶然というものは、そなたたちの物質世界の
38 de la vostra matera non si stende, of your material world, 書冊の外には及ばぬが、
39 tutta è dipinta nel cospetto etterno; is all depicted in the eternal sight; 永遠の御前にはすべて描かれている。
40 necessità però quindi non prende yet from this it takes no necessity, しかし、それによって必然性を帯びることはない、
41 se non come dal viso in che si specchia any more than from the eye that mirrors it ちょうど、川を下る船が、それを映す眼差しから
42 nave che per torrente giù discende. a ship that descends with the current. 必然性を帯びないのと同じようにな。
43 Da indi, sì come viene ad orecchia From there, just as sweet harmony from an organ そこから、オルガンの甘美な響きが
44 dolce armonia da organo, mi viene comes to the ear, there comes to my sight 耳に届くように、私には
45 a vista il tempo che ti s’apparecchia. the time that is being prepared for you. そなたに用意されている時が見えるのだ。
46 Qual si partio Ipolito d’Atene As Hippolytus departed from Athens ヒッポリュトスがアテネを去ったように、
47 per la spietata e perfida noverca, because of his pitiless and perfidious stepmother, 無慈悲で不実な継母によって、
48 tal di Fiorenza partir ti convene. so from Florence you must depart. そのようにそなたはフィレンツェを去らねばならぬ。
49 Questo si vuole e questo già si cerca, This is willed, and this is already sought, これは望まれ、これはすでに企まれている、
50 e tosto verrà fatto a chi ciò pensa and soon it will be accomplished by him who plots it そして、これを企む者によって速やかに実行されるだろう、
51 là dove Cristo tutto dì si merca. there where Christ is bought and sold all day. キリストが日々売り買いされる場所で。
52 La colpa seguirà la parte offensa The blame will follow the injured party 非難は、常のごとく、傷つけられた側に
53 in grido, come suol; ma la vendetta in the outcry, as is the custom; but vengeance 声高に付きまとうだろう。しかし、復讐は
54 fia testimonio al ver che la dispensa. will be a witness to the truth that dispenses it. それを施す真実の証となるであろう。
55 Tu lascerai ogne cosa diletta You shall leave everything you love そなたは、最も愛しく
56 più caramente; e questo è quello strale most dearly; and this is the arrow 愛したすべてのものを残していく。そしてこれこそが
57 che l’arco de lo essilio pria saetta. that the bow of exile shoots first. 追放の弓が最初に放つ矢なのだ。
58 Tu proverai sì come sa di sale You shall prove how salty tastes そなたは味わうだろう、他人のパンがいかに
59 lo pane altrui, e come è duro calle the bread of others, and how hard a path it is 塩辛い味であるか、そして他人の家の階段を
60 lo scendere e ’l salir per l’altrui scale. to go down and up another’s stairs. 上り下りすることが、いかに辛い道であるかを。
61 E quel che più ti graverà le spalle, And that which will weigh most upon your shoulders そして、そなたの肩に最も重くのしかかるのは、
62 sarà la compagnia malvagia e scempia will be the wicked and senseless company 悪辣で愚かな仲間たちであろう、
63 con la qual tu cadrai in questa valle; with which you will fall into that valley; そなたがこの谷に落ち込むことになる
64 che tutta ingrata, tutta matta ed empia for all ungrateful, all mad and impious 彼らは皆、恩知らずで、狂気じみ、不信心となって
65 si farà contr’ a te; ma, poco appresso, it will turn against you; but, soon after, そなたに敵対するだろう。だが、ほどなくして
66 ella, non tu, n’avrà rossa la tempia. it, not you, will have its temples bloody. そなたではなく、彼らがこめかみを赤く染めるのだ。
67 Di sua bestialitate il suo processo Of its bestiality its outcome 彼らの獣性が、その行く末を
68 farà la prova; sì ch’a te fia bello will be the proof; so that it will be a thing of honor for you 証明するだろう。ゆえに、そなたにとっては
69 averti fatta parte per te stesso. to have made a party for yourself. 自ら一党を成したことが誉れとなろう。
70 Lo primo tuo refugio e ’l primo ostello Your first refuge and your first lodging そなたの最初の避難所、最初の宿は
71 sarà la cortesia del gran Lombardo will be the courtesy of the great Lombard あの大ロンバルド人の礼節であろう、
72 che ’n su la scala porta il santo uccello; who on the ladder bears the holy bird; 梯子の上に聖なる鳥を掲げる者のだ。
73 ch’in te avrà sì benigno riguardo, for he will have for you such benign regard, 彼はそなたに、かくも温情ある配慮を寄せ、
74 che del fare e del chieder, tra voi due, that of the doing and the asking, between you two, 与えることと求めること、そなたら二人の間では、
75 fia primo quel che tra li altri è più tardo. the first will be that which among others is slower. 他の者たちの間ではより遅い方が、先になるだろう。
76 Con lui vedrai colui che ’mpresso fue, With him you will see the one who was impressed, 彼と共に、そなたは見るだろう、刻印された者を、
77 nascendo, sì da questa stella forte, at his birth, so by this strong star, 生まれる時に、この強き星によって、
78 che notabili fier l’opere sue. that his deeds will be notable. その行いが注目すべきものとなるほどに。
79 Non se ne son le genti ancora accorte The people have not yet taken notice of him 人々はまだそれに気づいていない
80 per la novella età, ché pur nove anni because of his young age, for only nine years その若さゆえに、いまだ九年しか
81 son queste rote intorno di lui torte; have these spheres revolved around him; これらの天球は彼の周りを回っていないのだから。
82 ma pria che ’l Guasco l’alto Arrigo inganni, but before the Gascon deceives the high Henry, だが、ガスコーニュ人が高貴なエンリーコを欺く前に、
83 parran faville de la sua virtute sparks of his virtue will appear 彼の美徳の火花は現れるだろう
84 in non curar d’argento né d’affanni. in his disregard for silver and for toils. 金銭も労苦も意に介さぬ姿のうちに。
85 Le sue magnificenze conosciute His magnificences will be known 彼の壮麗な行いは知れ渡り
86 saranno ancora, sì che ’ suoi nemici still further, so that his enemies やがて、その敵たちでさえ
87 non ne potran tener le lingue mute. will not be able to keep their tongues silent about them. それについて舌を黙らせてはおけぬだろう。
88 A lui t’aspetta e a’ suoi benefici; Look to him and to his benefits; 彼を、そして彼の恩恵を期待せよ。
89 per lui fia trasmutata molta gente, through him many people will be transformed, 彼によって多くの人々の運命は変えられ、
90 cambiando condizion ricchi e mendici; changing condition, rich and mendicants; 富める者と貧しき者がその境遇を変えるだろう。
91 e portera’ne scritto ne la mente and you will bear this written in your mind そして、そなたは心に書き留めるであろう
92 di lui, e nol dirai»; e disse cose about him, and you will not tell it»; and he said things 彼のことを、そしてそれを語ってはならぬ」。そうして彼は言った、
93 incredibili a quei che fier presente. incredible to those who will be present. その場に居合わせる者には信じ難いことを。
94 Poi giunse: «Figlio, queste son le chiose Then he added: «Son, these are the glosses そして加えた、「子よ、これらが註解である
95 di quel che ti fu detto; ecco le ’nsidie on that which was told to you; behold the snares そなたに告げられたことの。見よ、これが陰謀だ
96 che dietro a pochi giri son nascose. that are hidden behind a few revolutions. いくつかの回転の後に隠されている。
97 Non vo’ però ch’a’ tuoi vicini invidie, Yet I do not wish you to envy your neighbors, しかし、そなたの隣人らを憎むことは望まぬ、
98 poscia che s’infutura la tua vita since your life is futured そなたの人生は未来へと続くのだから
99 vie più là che ’l punir di lor perfidie». far beyond the punishing of their perfidies». 彼らの裏切りの罰よりもはるか彼方へ」。
100 Poi che, tacendo, si mostrò spedita When, by its silence, the holy soul showed it had finished 沈黙して、完了したことを示されたので
101 l’anima santa di metter la trama putting the weft 聖なる魂が、緯糸を織り込むのを
102 in quella tela ch’io le porsi ordita, onto that warp I had offered it, 私が差し出した経糸の張られたその機に、
103 io cominciai, come colui che brama, I began, like one who longs, 私は語り始めた、切望する者のように、
104 dubitando, consiglio da persona while doubting, for counsel from a person 疑いながら、助言を、ある人物からの
105 che vede e vuol dirittamente e ama: who sees and wills rightly and loves: 見通し、正しく望み、そして愛する（人物からの）：
106 «Ben veggio, padre mio, sì come sprona "I see well, my father, how time spurs 「父よ、私はよく見ております、時がいかに私に向かって
107 lo tempo verso me, per colpo darmi toward me, to deal me a blow 鞭を打っているかを、私に一撃を与えるために
108 tal, ch’è più grave a chi più s’abbandona; such that it is heaviest for him who most gives way; それは、最も身を委ねる者にこそ最も重い一撃です。
109 per che di provedenza è buon ch’io m’armi, for which it is good that I arm myself with foresight, それゆえ、私が先見の明で武装するのは良いことです、
110 sì che, se loco m’è tolto più caro, so that, if my dearest place is taken from me, それで、もし最も愛する場所が私から奪われても、
111 io non perdessi li altri per miei carmi. I may not lose the others because of my poems. 私が自らの詩ゆえに他の場所を失わないように。
112 Giù per lo mondo sanza fine amaro, Down through the world of endless bitterness, 果てしなく苦い世界を下に、
113 e per lo monte del cui bel cacume and through the mountain from whose lovely summit そしてかの山の、その美しい頂から
114 li occhi de la mia donna mi levaro, the eyes of my lady lifted me, 我が貴婦人の瞳が私を引き上げてくださった山を、
115 e poscia per lo ciel, di lume in lume, and then through heaven, from light to light, そしてその後は天を、光から光へと、
116 ho io appreso quel che s’io ridico, I have learned that which, if I retell it, 私は学びました、それをもし私が語り直せば、
117 a molti fia sapor di forte agrume; will for many have the taste of a harsh acid; 多くの者にとって強い苦味の味わいとなるでしょう。
118 e s’io al vero son timido amico, and if I am a timid friend to the truth, そしてもし私が真実に対して臆病な友であるならば、
119 temo di perder viver tra coloro I fear to lose my life among those 私は人々の間で生きるのを失うことを恐れます
120 che questo tempo chiameranno antico». who will call this time ancient." この時代を古い時代と呼ぶであろう（人々の間で）。
121 La luce in che rideva il mio tesoro The light in which my treasure smiled, 私の宝が微笑んでいたその光は
122 ch’io trovai lì, si fé prima corusca, whom I found there, first became scintillating, 私がそこで見出した（光は）、まずきらめき、
123 quale a raggio di sole specchio d’oro; like a golden mirror in a sunbeam; 太陽の光を受けた黄金の鏡のように。
124 indi rispuose: «Coscïenza fusca then it answered: "A conscience dark そして答えた、「暗い良心は
125 o de la propria o de l’altrui vergogna either from its own or from another's shame 自らのあるいは他者の恥によって
126 pur sentirà la tua parola brusca. will surely feel your speech to be harsh. やはりあなたの辛辣な言葉を感じるだろう。
127 Ma nondimen, rimossa ogne menzogna, But nonetheless, all falsehood set aside, しかしながら、一切の偽りを退け、
128 tutta tua visïon fa manifesta; make your entire vision manifest; あなたのヴィジョンを全て明らかにせよ。
129 e lascia pur grattar dov’ è la rogna. and just let them scratch where the itch is. そして痒いところは掻かせておけばよい。
130 Ché se la voce tua sarà molesta For if your voice shall be offensive なぜなら、あなたの声がたとえ不快であっても
131 nel primo gusto, vital nodrimento at the first taste, vital nourishment 最初の味わいにおいて、生命の糧を
132 lascerà poi, quando sarà digesta. it will leave behind, when it has been digested. 後には残すだろう、それが消化された時には。
133 Questo tuo grido farà come vento, This cry of yours will do as does the wind, あなたのこの叫びは風のようになるだろう、
134 che le più alte cime più percuote; which strikes the highest peaks the most; それは最も高い峰を最も強く打つ。
135 e ciò non fa d’onor poco argomento. and this is no small argument for honor. そしてこれは、名誉にとってささやかな根拠ではない。
136 Però ti son mostrate in queste rote, For this reason there have been shown to you in these wheels, それゆえ、あなたにはこれらの天輪や、
137 nel monte e ne la valle dolorosa on the mountain, and in the sorrowful valley, 山や、苦悩の谷において示されたのだ
138 pur l’anime che son di fama note, only those souls who are of famous note, ただ名声の知られた魂たちだけが、
139 che l’animo di quel ch’ode, non posa because the mind of one who hears does not rest なぜなら、聞く者の魂は、安らぎも
140 né ferma fede per essempro ch’aia nor fix its faith on an example that has 固い信仰も持たないからだ、ある手本によっては
141 la sua radice incognita e ascosa, its root unknown and hidden, その根が知られず隠されている（手本では）、
142 né per altro argomento che non paia». nor on any other proof that is not apparent." また、明らかでない他の論拠によっても」。
