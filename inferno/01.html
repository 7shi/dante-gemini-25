Inferno — Canto 1

Lost in a dark wood and blocked from a sunlit hill by three beasts, the narrator is rescued by the poet Virgil, who offers to guide him on a journey through Hell. 人生の道に迷い、三頭の獣に阻まれた語り手の前に詩人ウェルギリウスが現れ、地獄を通る別の道へと導くことを申し出る。

1 Nel mezzo del cammin di nostra vita Midway upon the journey of our life 我らの人生の道の半ばで
2 mi ritrovai per una selva oscura, I found myself in a dark wood, 私は暗い森の中に自分を見出した、
3 ché la diritta via era smarrita. for the straight way was lost. 正しい道がそこでは見失われていたからだ。
4 Ahi quanto a dir qual era è cosa dura Ah, how hard a thing it is to say what it was, ああ、それがどのようなものであったか語るのは何と難しいことか
5 esta selva selvaggia e aspra e forte this savage and harsh and strong wood この野蛮で、険しく、力強い森は
6 che nel pensier rinova la paura! that in thought renews the fear! 思い出すだに恐怖が蘇る！
7 Tant’ è amara che poco è più morte; It is so bitter that death is little more; それはあまりに苦く、死もそれに僅かに勝るのみ；
8 ma per trattar del ben ch’i’ vi trovai, but to treat of the good that I found there, しかし、そこで私が見出した善について語るため、
9 dirò de l’altre cose ch’i’ v’ho scorte. I will tell of the other things I saw within. 私はそこで見た他の事々について語ろう。
10 Io non so ben ridir com’ i’ v’intrai, I cannot well recount how I entered it, 私がいかにしてそこへ入ったのか、よくは言えない、
11 tant’ era pien di sonno a quel punto so full of sleep was I at that point その時、私はあまりに眠気に満ちていたので
12 che la verace via abbandonai. when I abandoned the true way. 真の道を捨て去ってしまったのだ。
13 Ma poi ch’i’ fui al piè d’un colle giunto, But after I had reached the foot of a hill, しかし、とある丘の麓に着いた時、
14 là dove terminava quella valle there where that valley ended かの谷が終わるその場所で
15 che m’avea di paura il cor compunto, which had pierced my heart with fear, それは私の心を恐怖で突き刺していたのだが、
16 guardai in alto e vidi le sue spalle I looked on high and saw its shoulders 私は見上げ、その肩が
17 vestite già de’ raggi del pianeta already clothed with the rays of the planet すでに惑星の光に覆われているのを見た
18 che mena dritto altrui per ogne calle. that leads others straight along every path. それは人をあらゆる道で正しく導く。
19 Allor fu la paura un poco queta, Then the fear was somewhat quieted, その時、恐怖は少し和らいだ、
20 che nel lago del cor m’era durata which in the lake of my heart had lasted 心の湖に、続いていたのだ、
21 la notte ch’i’ passai con tanta pieta. the night that I passed with so much anguish. 私がかくも苦悩のうちに過ごした夜の間。
22 E come quei che con lena affannata, And as one who with labored breath, そして、息を切らしながら、
23 uscito fuor del pelago a la riva, having come forth from the sea to the shore, 大海から岸辺へと逃れ出で、
24 si volge a l’acqua perigliosa e guata, turns to the perilous water and gazes, 危険な水面を振り返り、じっと見つめる者のように、
25 così l’animo mio, ch’ancor fuggiva, so my soul, which was still fleeing, そのように私の魂も、なおも逃れ続けてはいたが、
26 si volse a retro a rimirar lo passo turned back to look again upon the pass 振り返り、かの道筋を見つめ返した
27 che non lasciò già mai persona viva. which had never yet let a living person leave. 生きて通り抜けた者をかつて一人も許さなかった道を。
28 Poi ch’èi posato un poco il corpo lasso, After I had rested my weary body a little, 疲れた体を少し休ませてから、
29 ripresi via per la piaggia diserta, I took up my way again along the desert slope, 私は再び人気のない斜面を進み始めた、
30 sì che ’l piè fermo sempre era ’l più basso. so that the firm foot was always the lower. 軸足が常に下側になるようにして。
31 Ed ecco, quasi al cominciar de l’erta, And behold, almost at the start of the steep slope, すると見よ、ほとんど坂道の始まりで、
32 una lonza leggera e presta molto, a leopard, light and very swift, 一頭の豹が、身軽で非常に素早く、
33 che di pel macolato era coverta; which was covered with a spotted hide; 斑らの毛皮に覆われていた；
34 e non mi si partia dinanzi al volto, and it would not depart from before my face, そして私の顔の前から去ろうとせず、
35 anzi ’mpediva tanto il mio cammino, rather it so impeded my path, それどころか私の道をひどく妨げたので、
36 ch’i’ fui per ritornar più volte vòlto. that I was many times turned to go back. 私は幾度も引き返そうと向き直った。
37 Temp’ era dal principio del mattino, It was the time of the beginning of the morning, 時は朝の始まりであり、
38 e ’l sol montava ’n sù con quelle stelle and the sun was mounting up with those stars そして太陽はあの星々とともに昇っていた
39 ch’eran con lui quando l’amor divino that were with him when the divine love 神の愛が最初に
40 mosse di prima quelle cose belle; first moved those beautiful things; あの美しいものたちを動かした時に彼と共にあった；
41 sì ch’a bene sperar m’era cagione so that to hope well it was for me a cause それゆえに、希望を抱かせる要因となったのは
42 di quella fiera a la gaetta pelle of that beast with the gaudy hide あの華やかな皮を持つ獣に対して
43 l’ora del tempo e la dolce stagione; the hour of the time and the sweet season; その時刻と心地よい季節であった；
44 ma non sì che paura non mi desse but not so that fear was not given me by しかし、私に恐怖を与えぬほどではなかった
45 la vista che m’apparve d’un leone. the sight that appeared to me of a lion. 私の前に現れた一頭の獅子の光景は。
46 Questi parea che contra me venisse This one seemed to be coming against me こいつは私に向かって来るように見えた
47 con la test’ alta e con rabbiosa fame, with its head high and with rabid hunger, 頭を高く上げ、猛烈な飢えを抱いて、
48 sì che parea che l’aere ne tremesse. so that it seemed the air trembled from it. それゆえ大気がそれによって震えるかのようであった。
49 Ed una lupa, che di tutte brame And a she-wolf, that with all longings そして一頭の雌狼が、あらゆる欲望で
50 sembiava carca ne la sua magrezza, seemed laden in her leanness, その痩身に満ちているように見え、
51 e molte genti fé già viver grame, and many peoples has already made live wretchedly, そして多くの民をすでに惨めに生かしてきた、
52 questa mi porse tanto di gravezza this one placed on me so much gravity こいつは私にあまりの重圧を与えた
53 con la paura ch’uscia di sua vista, with the fear that issued from its sight, その姿から発する恐怖によって、
54 ch’io perdei la speranza de l’altezza. that I lost the hope of the height. 私は高みへの希望を失った。
55 E qual è quei che volontieri acquista, And as is he who willingly acquires, そして喜んで獲得する者、
56 e giugne ’l tempo che perder lo face, and the time comes that makes him lose, そしてそれを失わせる時が来て、
57 che ’n tutti suoi pensier piange e s’attrista; that in all his thoughts he weeps and saddens; あらゆる思いの中で泣き悲しむ者；
58 tal mi fece la bestia sanza pace, such did the beast without peace make me, そのように私をさせた、あの安らぎなき獣は、
59 che, venendomi ’ncontro, a poco a poco which, coming against me, little by little 私に向かって来ながら、少しずつ
60 mi ripigneva là dove ’l sol tace. drove me back there where the sun is silent. 私を太陽が沈黙する場所へと押し戻した。
61 Mentre ch’i’ rovinava in basso loco, While I was ruining into a low place, 私が低い場所へと転がり落ちていた時、
62 dinanzi a li occhi mi si fu offerto before my eyes there was offered to me 私の目の前に現れたのは、
63 chi per lungo silenzio parea fioco. one who through long silence seemed faint. 長い沈黙ゆえにかすかに見える者であった。
64 Quando vidi costui nel gran diserto, When I saw him in the great desert, この広大な荒野で彼を見た時、
65 «Miserere di me», gridai a lui, "Have mercy on me," I cried to him, 「私を憐れみたまえ」と私は彼に叫んだ、
66 «qual che tu sii, od ombra od omo certo!». "whatever you may be, or shade or certain man!". 「あなたが何者であれ、影であれ、確かな人間であれ！」と。
67 Rispuosemi: «Non omo, omo già fui, He answered me: "Not man, man I once was, 彼は私に答えた、「人間ではない、かつては人間だったが、
68 e li parenti miei furon lombardi, and my parents were Lombards, そして私の両親はロンバルディア人で、
69 mantoani per patrïa ambedui. Mantuans by homeland both. 二人ともマントヴァを故郷としていた。
70 Nacqui sub Iulio, ancor che fosse tardi, I was born under Julius, though it was late, 私はユリウスのもとに生まれた、その治世の末期であったが、
71 e vissi a Roma sotto ’l buono Augusto and I lived in Rome under the good Augustus そして善良なるアウグストゥスの下、ローマに生きた、
72 nel tempo de li dèi falsi e bugiardi. in the time of the false and lying gods. 偽りの、嘘つきの神々の時代に。
73 Poeta fui, e cantai di quel giusto A poet I was, and I sang of that just 私は詩人であった、そしてあの正義の
74 figliuol d’Anchise che venne di Troia, son of Anchises who came from Troy, アンキセスの息子について歌った、彼はトロイアから来た、
75 poi che ’l superbo Ilïón fu combusto. after proud Ilion was burnt. 誇り高きイリオンが焼き尽くされた後に。
76 Ma tu perché ritorni a tanta noia? But you, why do you return to so much grief? しかし、あなたは何故これほどの苦悩に戻るのか？
77 perché non sali il dilettoso monte why do you not climb the delightful mountain なぜ喜びに満ちた山に登らないのか、
78 ch’è principio e cagion di tutta gioia?». that is the beginning and cause of all joy?". それはあらゆる喜びの始まりであり、原因であるのに？」
79 «Or se’ tu quel Virgilio e quella fonte "Now are you that Virgil and that fount 「では、あなたはあのウェルギリウス、そしてあの泉なのですか、
80 che spandi di parlar sì largo fiume?», that spills forth so broad a river of speech?", これほど広大な言葉の川を広げる？」と、
81 rispuos’ io lui con vergognosa fronte. I answered him with a bashful brow. 私は彼に恥じらいの面持ちで答えた。
82 «O de li altri poeti onore e lume, "O of the other poets honor and light, 「おお、他の詩人たちの名誉にして光よ、
83 vagliami ’l lungo studio e ’l grande amore may the long study and the great love avail me 長年の研究と大いなる愛が私の助けとなりますように、
84 che m’ha fatto cercar lo tuo volume. that has made me search your volume. それが私にあなたの書物を探させたのです。
85 Tu se’ lo mio maestro e ’l mio autore, You are my master and my author, あなたは私の師であり、私の手本です、
86 tu se’ solo colui da cu’ io tolsi you are the only one from whom I took あなたこそ私がそこから学んだ唯一の人、
87 lo bello stilo che m’ha fatto onore. the beautiful style that has brought me honor. 私に名誉をもたらした美しき文体を。
88 Vedi la bestia per cu’ io mi volsi; See the beast for which I turned back; 私が引き返した原因の獣をご覧なさい。
89 aiutami da lei, famoso saggio, help me from her, famous sage, あの獣から私を助けてください、名高き賢者よ、
90 ch’ella mi fa tremar le vene e i polsi». for she makes my veins and pulses tremble". あれは私の血管と脈を震わせるのです」
91 «A te convien tenere altro vïaggio», «For you it is fitting to take another journey», 「汝には別の旅路を取ることがふさわしい」
92 rispuose, poi che lagrimar mi vide, he answered, when he saw me weeping, 私が涙するのを見て、彼は答えた、
93 «se vuo’ campar d’esto loco selvaggio; «if you wish to escape from this savage place; 「もしこの荒れ果てた場所から逃れたいのであれば。
94 ché questa bestia, per la qual tu gride, for this beast, for which you cry out, なぜなら、汝が叫び声を上げる原因であるこの獣は、
95 non lascia altrui passar per la sua via, does not let others pass by her way, 他の者がその道を通るのを許さず、
96 ma tanto lo ’mpedisce che l’uccide; but so impedes him that she kills him; 彼を妨げて殺してしまうほどなのだ。
97 e ha natura sì malvagia e ria, and has a nature so malicious and evil, そして、その性質はかくも邪悪で悪辣であり、
98 che mai non empie la bramosa voglia, that she never sates her greedy craving, 飽くなき欲望を決して満たすことなく、
99 e dopo ’l pasto ha più fame che pria. and after the meal has more hunger than before. 食後には以前よりも飢えているのだ。
100 Molti son li animali a cui s’ammoglia, Many are the animals to which she weds, 多くの獣がこれと交わり、
101 e più saranno ancora, infin che ’l veltro and there will be more still, until the Greyhound さらに多くなるだろう、やがて猟犬が
102 verrà, che la farà morir con doglia. shall come, who will make her die with grief. やって来て、苦痛のうちにこれを死に至らしめるまで。
103 Questi non ciberà terra né peltro, He will not feed on land nor pewter, この者は土もしろがねも糧とせず、
104 ma sapïenza, amore e virtute, but wisdom, love, and virtue, 知恵と愛と徳を糧とするだろう。
105 e sua nazion sarà tra feltro e feltro. and his nation will be between felt and felt. そしてその生まれはフェルトとフェルトの間であろう。
106 Di quella umile Italia fia salute Of that humble Italy he shall be the salvation あの卑しきイタリアの救いとなるだろう、
107 per cui morì la vergine Cammilla, for which died the virgin Camilla, そのために処女カミッラ、
108 Eurialo e Turno e Niso di ferute. Euryalus and Turnus and Nisus of wounds. エウリュアロスとトゥルヌスとニソスは傷に倒れた。
109 Questi la caccerà per ogne villa, He will hunt her through every town, この者は全ての町からかの獣を追い払い、
110 fin che l’avrà rimessa ne lo ’nferno, until he has sent her back into Hell, ついに地獄へと送り返すだろう、
111 là onde ’nvidia prima dipartilla. there whence envy first set her loose. 嫉妬が最初にそこから送り出した場所へ。
112 Ond’ io per lo tuo me’ penso e discerno Therefore for your own good I think and I discern それゆえ、汝のために私は考え、見極める、
113 che tu mi segui, e io sarò tua guida, that you follow me, and I will be your guide, 汝が私に従い、私が汝の導き手となることを。
114 e trarrotti di qui per loco etterno; and draw you from here through an eternal place; そして、ここから汝を永遠の場所へと連れ出そう。
115 ove udirai le disperate strida, where you shall hear the desperate cries, そこでは汝は絶望の叫びを聞き、
116 vedrai li antichi spiriti dolenti, see the ancient sorrowful spirits, 苦悩する古の霊たちを見るだろう、
117 ch’a la seconda morte ciascun grida; each of whom cries out for the second death; 彼らは皆、第二の死を求めて叫んでいる。
118 e vederai color che son contenti and you will see those who are content そして汝は満ち足りている者たちを見るだろう
119 nel foco, perché speran di venire in the fire, because they hope to come, 炎の中で。なぜなら彼らはやがて至ることを望むからだ
120 quando che sia a le beate genti. whenever it may be, to the blessed people. いつかは、祝福された人々のもとへ。
121 A le quai poi se tu vorrai salire, To whom if you should then wish to ascend, その後、もし汝が彼らのもとへ昇りたいと望むなら、
122 anima fia a ciò più di me degna: a soul shall be for that more worthy than I: それには私よりもふさわしい魂がいるだろう。
123 con lei ti lascerò nel mio partire; with her I will leave you at my departure; 私が去る時には、汝を彼女に託そう。
124 ché quello imperador che là sù regna, for that emperor who reigns up there, なぜなら、かの高みで統べる皇帝は、
125 perch’ i’ fu’ ribellante a la sua legge, because I was a rebel to his law, 私がその法に背いた者であったために、
126 non vuol che ’n sua città per me si vegna. does not wish that one come into his city through me. 私の市に私を通して入ることを望まないのだ。
127 In tutte parti impera e quivi regge; In all parts he rules and there he keeps his seat; 全ての場所を彼は支配し、そこを治める。
128 quivi è la sua città e l’alto seggio: there is his city and his high throne: そこに彼の都と高き玉座がある。
129 oh felice colui cu’ ivi elegge!». oh happy the one whom he elects there!». ああ、幸いなるかな、彼がそこに選ぶ者は！」。
130 E io a lui: «Poeta, io ti richeggio And I to him: «Poet, I request of you そして私は彼に言った。「詩人よ、私はあなたに懇願します、
131 per quello Dio che tu non conoscesti, by that God whom you did not know, あなたが知ることのなかったかの神によって、
132 acciò ch’io fugga questo male e peggio, so that I may flee this evil and worse, 私がこの災いと、より悪しきものから逃れるために、
133 che tu mi meni là dov’ or dicesti, that you lead me there where you just said, あなたが今おっしゃった場所へ私を導いてくださることを。
134 sì ch’io veggia la porta di san Pietro so that I may see the gate of Saint Peter そうすれば私は聖ペテロの門と、
135 e color cui tu fai cotanto mesti». and those whom you make out to be so sad». あなたがかくも悲しげに語る者たちを見ることができるでしょう」。
136 Allor si mosse, e io li tenni dietro. Then he moved, and I kept behind him. すると彼は動き出し、私はその後ろに従った。
