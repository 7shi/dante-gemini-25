Inferno — Canto 33

After hearing Count Ugolino's gruesome tale of starvation, Dante meets Friar Alberigo, who explains that the souls of traitors to guests are damned to hell while their bodies live on Earth possessed by demons. ウゴリーノ伯が飢えによる凄惨な死の物語を語った後、ダンテは肉体が地上にあるまま魂が地獄に落ちた裏切り者たちがいるトロメア区へと進む。

1 La bocca sollevò dal fiero pasto His mouth he lifted from the savage meal, その口を、むごたらしい食事から上げ、
2 quel peccator, forbendola a’ capelli that sinner, wiping it on the hair かの罪人は、それを髪で拭った、
3 del capo ch’elli avea di retro guasto. of the head that he had ravaged from behind. 後ろから自分が食い尽くしていた頭の。
4 Poi cominciò: «Tu vuo’ ch’io rinovelli Then he began: “You want me to renew それから始めた。「そなたは私に新たにさせたいのか、
5 disperato dolor che ’l cor mi preme a desperate grief that presses on my heart 心を締め付ける絶望の苦しみを、
6 già pur pensando, pria ch’io ne favelli. just thinking of it, before I even speak. それを口にする前に、ただ思うだけでも。
7 Ma se le mie parole esser dien seme But if my words are to be the seed だが、もし我が言葉が種となり、
8 che frutti infamia al traditor ch’i’ rodo, that bears fruit of infamy for the traitor whom I gnaw, 私が噛み砕くこの裏切り者に不名誉の実を結ぶなら、
9 parlar e lagrimar vedrai insieme. you will see me speak and weep at the same time. 語ることと涙することを、そなたは同時に見るだろう。
10 Io non so chi tu se’ né per che modo I do not know who you are nor by what means 私はそなたが誰か、いかなる方法で
11 venuto se’ qua giù; ma fiorentino you have come down here; but a Florentine ここへ下りて来たか知らぬ。だがフィレンツェの者と
12 mi sembri veramente quand’ io t’odo. you truly seem to me when I hear you. そなたの言葉を聞けば、まことにそう思われる。
13 Tu dei saper ch’i’ fui conte Ugolino, You must know that I was Count Ugolino, 知るがよい、私はウゴリーノ伯であり、
14 e questi è l’arcivescovo Ruggieri: and this is the Archbishop Ruggieri: そしてこやつはルッジエーリ大司教だ。
15 or ti dirò perché i son tal vicino. now I will tell you why I am such a neighbor. なぜ私がこれほど近くにいるのか、今から話そう。
16 Che per l’effetto de’ suo’ mai pensieri, That by the effect of his evil thoughts, こやつの邪な考えの結果として、
17 fidandomi di lui, io fossi preso trusting in him, I was taken こやつを信じた私が捕らえられ、
18 e poscia morto, dir non è mestieri; and then put to death, there is no need to say; その後殺されたことは、語るには及ばぬ。
19 però quel che non puoi avere inteso, but that which you cannot have heard, しかし、そなたが聞いているはずのないこと、
20 cioè come la morte mia fu cruda, that is, how cruel my death was, すなわち、我が死がいかに惨いものであったか、
21 udirai, e saprai s’e’ m’ha offeso. you will hear, and you will know if he has wronged me. それを聞けば、こやつが私を辱めたか分かるだろう。

23 la qual per me ha ’l titol de la fame, which because of me has the title of Hunger, それは私ゆえに『飢餓』の称号を得たのだが、
24 e che conviene ancor ch’altrui si chiuda, and in which others must yet be shut, そして今後も他の者が閉じ込められるべき場所だが、

71 vid’ io cascar li tre ad uno ad uno I saw the three fall one by one 私は見たのだ、三人が一人また一人と倒れるのを

73 già cieco, a brancolar sovra ciascuno, already blind, to grope over each one, すでに盲目となって、一人一人の上を手探りすることを。
74 e due dì li chiamai, poi che fur morti. and for two days I called them, after they were dead. そして二日間彼らを呼び続けた、彼らが死んだ後も。
75 Poscia, più che ’l dolor, poté ’l digiuno». Then, more than grief, hunger had power.» その後、悲しみよりも、飢えが勝ったのだ」。
76 Quand’ ebbe detto ciò, con li occhi torti When he had said that, with eyes askew それを語り終えると、彼は歪んだ目で
77 riprese ’l teschio misero co’ denti, he seized the wretched skull again with his teeth, 哀れな頭蓋骨を再び歯で捉えた、
78 che furo a l’osso, come d’un can, forti. which were, to the bone, as strong as a dog’s. その歯は骨に達し、犬のように、強かった。

80 del bel paese là dove ’l sì suona, of the fair country where the ‘sì’ sounds, 『スィ』の音が響く美しき国の、
81 poi che i vicini a te punir son lenti, since your neighbors are slow to punish you, 汝の隣人たちが罰を与えるに遅いからには、
82 muovasi la Capraia e la Gorgona, let Capraia and Gorgona move カプライアとゴルゴーナよ動け、
83 e faccian siepe ad Arno in su la foce, and make a hedge for the Arno at its mouth, そしてアルノ川の河口に垣をなし、
84 sì ch’elli annieghi in te ogne persona! so that it may drown every person in you! 汝の中のすべての人を溺れさせよ！
85 Che se ’l conte Ugolino aveva voce For if Count Ugolino had the name たとえウゴリーノ伯に声があろうと

87 non dovei tu i figliuoi porre a tal croce. you should not have put his sons to such a cross. 汝はその子らをかくも残酷な十字架にかけるべきではなかった。

89 novella Tebe, Uguiccione e ’l Brigata O new Thebes, Uguiccione and Brigata 新たなテーベよ、ウグッチオーネとブリガータを
90 e li altri due che ’l canto suso appella. and the other two whom the canto names above. そして先の詩が呼びかけた他の二人を。
91 Noi passammo oltre, là ’ve la gelata We passed on, to where the frost 我らはさらに先へ進んだ、そこでは氷が
92 ruvidamente un’altra gente fascia, harshly binds another people, 無慈悲に別の一群を縛りつけ、
93 non volta in giù, ma tutta riversata. not turned downward, but all thrown on their backs. 下を向かず、皆仰向けになっていた。
94 Lo pianto stesso lì pianger non lascia, The weeping itself there does not let them weep, 涙そのものがそこで泣くことを許さず、
95 e ’l duol che truova in su li occhi rintoppo, and the grief that finds a block upon the eyes そして目に妨げを見出す悲しみは、
96 si volge in entro a far crescer l’ambascia; turns inward to make the anguish grow; 内に向かって苦悩を増大させる。

99 rïempion sotto ’l ciglio tutto il coppo. they fill up the whole socket beneath the brow. 眉の下の眼窩をすっかり満たすからだ。

151 Ahi Genovesi, uomini diversi Ah Genoese, men estranged ああ、ジェノヴァ人よ、人々は異なる
152 d’ogne costume e pien d’ogne magagna, from every custom and full of all corruption, あらゆる慣習から、そしてあらゆる欠陥に満ちた、
153 perché non siete voi del mondo spersi? why are you not scattered from the world? なぜ汝らは世界から追い払われないのか？
154 Ché col peggiore spirto di Romagna For with the worst spirit of Romagna なぜならロマーニャの最悪の魂と共に
155 trovai di voi un tal, che per sua opra I found one of you, such that for his deed 私は汝らの一人を見つけた、その行いのために
156 in anima in Cocito già si bagna, in soul already bathes in Cocytus, 魂はすでにコキュートスに浸かり、
157 e in corpo par vivo ancor di sopra. and in body still seems alive above. そして体はまだ上で生きているように見える。
