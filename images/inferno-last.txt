A powerful and hopeful image depicting the final moment of Dante's Inferno. Two figures, the poets Virgil and Dante, are emerging from a dark, narrow cavern. They are climbing up a rough, hidden path next to a small, glistening stream that has carved its way through the rock. The opening of the cavern reveals a breathtaking, clear night sky filled with brilliant, twinkling stars. The focus is on the contrast between the oppressive darkness of the underground passage they are leaving behind and the liberating, beautiful expanse of the cosmos they are re-entering. The mood is one of profound relief and awe.
