An epic, ethereal fantasy painting for the title image of Dante's "Purgatorio." The scene depicts the colossal Mount Purgatory, a single, massive, terraced mountain spiraling up from a calm ocean into a serene dawn sky. The light is soft and hopeful, with the morning star and four other brilliant stars (the cardinal virtues) visible. Two small figures, Dante and Virgil, are seen on the shore at the base, about to begin their ascent. The path winds upward, with each terrace subtly hinting at its nature. Far above, the summit is crowned with the lush, glowing greenery of the Earthly Paradise. Ethereal angels, glowing with soft light, can be seen guarding the way. The style should combine the majestic, light-filled landscapes of the Hudson River School with the spiritual and symbolic quality of William Blake's art. The overall mood is one of immense scale, serene beauty, and profound hope.
