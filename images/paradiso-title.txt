An abstract and luminous cosmic painting for the title image of Dante's "Paradiso." The composition is a journey into pure, divine light, structured by sacred geometry. In the foreground, the ethereal figures of Dante and Beatrice are being drawn into a vortex of concentric, glowing spheres representing the heavens. Thousands of souls appear as brilliant motes of light, forming complex, symmetrical patterns within the spheres: a celestial cross, a divine eagle, and finally, the immense, multi-tiered Celestial Rose, a cosmic mandala of saints. The background culminates in a single point of unbearable, white-hot brilliance—the vision of God—from which all light emanates. The style should be a blend of geometric abstraction, Hubble Space Telescope imagery, and the spiritual mandalas of Eastern art, rendered with a sense of infinite depth and transcendent joy. The colors are vibrant, pure, and overwhelmingly bright.
