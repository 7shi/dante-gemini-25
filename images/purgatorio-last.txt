An ethereal and serene scene from the end of Dante's Purgatorio. In the heart of the sunlit Earthly Paradise, the beautiful Matelda gently leads Dante and Statius to the sacred river Eunoë. Dante is depicted drinking from the clear, life-giving water, or just raising his head, his face filled with a sense of profound renewal and purity. The atmosphere is bathed in a warm, divine light, surrounded by lush, vibrant foliage. The image should convey a feeling of spiritual rebirth, as if he is a new plant, now pure and ready to ascend to the stars.
