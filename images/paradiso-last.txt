An abstract and sublime representation of Dante's final vision of God. The image is dominated by three vast, luminous, concentric circles of three different, brilliant colors, yet all sharing the same dimension, representing the Holy Trinity. The second, middle circle miraculously holds the faint, ethereal image of a human form, symbolizing the Incarnation. The entire composition should radiate an overwhelming, ineffable divine light and a sense of perfect, cosmic harmony. This is not a literal scene, but a geometric and mystical vision of the Love that moves the sun and the other stars.
