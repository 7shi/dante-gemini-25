An epic, dark fantasy digital painting for the title image of Dante's "Inferno." The composition is an ultra-wide, awe-inspiring view looking down into the vast, conical abyss of Hell. Two small figures, Dante and Virgil, stand on a rocky outcrop in the foreground, dwarfed by the immense scale. The spiraling circles of Hell descend into darkness, each level hinting at different torments: rivers of fire, a forest of gnarled trees, burning plains, and monstrous architecture. At the deepest point, the colossal, three-faced figure of Lucifer is visible, trapped in the frozen lake of Cocytus. The style should be a fusion of the dramatic chiaroscuro of Gustave Doré's engravings and the surreal, nightmarish landscapes of Zdzisław Beksiński. The lighting is cinematic and terrifying, with a powerful sense of dread, despair, and monumental scale.
